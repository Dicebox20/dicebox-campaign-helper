## Campaign premise and status

The exact title is **Cabin in the Ashen Veil**. It began as a Halloween one-shot idea and expanded into a multi-session horror campaign in Neverwinter Wood, roughly 1501 DR, around fifty years after the 1451 DR Mount Hotenow catastrophe. Strangers arrive at an isolated estate during a storm. The current plan has two independent mysteries:

1. Maldrath Voss's active ritual and the approach of Vharzagul, an original **yagnoloth/yugoloth**, not a demon.
2. The older family tragedy and five sentient haunts, whose motives should be inferred through clues rather than explained to players.

The haunt mystery is optional for completing the ritual objective. Stopping Voss does not end the older haunting. The design is not Foundry-ready: no current Foundry runtime verification, production scene certification, or repository/KB sync is included in this recovery.

## Setting, family, and haunt canon

- The father was a Gond-admiring stonemason and tinkerer who built the house and its mechanisms. The mother was a half-elf artist and cleric of Sune Firehair. Their daughter Sophia became blind after a severe childhood illness. The family lost an unborn child through miscarriage.
- Campaign interpretation: during the 1451 DR ash-zombie disaster, the father's protective will became infused in the handcrafted house through supernatural force associated with the Dread Ring and the Ruining. The Dread Ring supplies/amplifies force; the father's will gives the house its purpose. This is original campaign lore, not a claim about an exact published-lore timestamp. The father is not a separate ghost speaking through the building.
- Sune's Grove is a carefully built woodland sanctuary integrated with roots, vines, trees, and overgrowth. One grave for the miscarried child is now accepted; older “no graves” and generic cemetery descriptions are superseded. The Sune statue's outstretched hands are reserved for the late Scrying Bowl. A separate concealed embossed sliding relief is on the statue base.
- The five haunts are: **The Crooked Man** (Donovan, groundskeeper trying to wrangle children to safety); **The Eyeless Girl** (Sophia, longing to see again); **The Shadow That Sees** (the mother, a source-less flat feminine wall shadow with one bloodshot eye, seeking admiration); **The Null Child** (incomplete consciousness of the miscarried child projected in cosmic-horror form); and **The House That Loves** (the father's protective will in the house). None is automatically harmless. The Null Child's mouth is a visual horror feature, not the source of a separate “Truth” effect.
- The family's story is not to be delivered as a lore dump. Approved environmental clues include a cheerful portrait of Donovan gardening, Sophia's coloring books, and an unused pristine-but-dusty nursery. During the Crimson Veil, the house may project urgent protective memories; stage-specific details remain partly open.
- At the start, the Crooked Man may chase/capture at most one player at a time under the Sophia-bedroom release procedure. During the Crimson Veil he stops pursuing/capturing players and attacks demons instead. The Shadow may manifest openly and grant a protection buff after the Veil; exact rules remain open. The house never speaks explanatory dialogue. Sophia sometimes hides by her mother's leg and may cradle the Null Child after death; she was not his older sister in life.

## Current progression and dependency summary

### Checkpoints

- **Morning, about 9 AM:** arrival and early cabin, pond, and Grove exploration.
- **Early finale, roughly one-third:** obtain the Brass Key from the Sune relief stash and open the Study.
- **Mid-game, about 2 PM:** use Study note `587` to open Donovan's shack box, operate the sluice, retrieve the Weathered Key, enter the basement, and open the Aqueduct; first Crooked Man chase leads toward the forest well/maze.
- **Late-game, about two-thirds and nightfall:** on a later return to the Grove, approach the northwest bust and trigger the second Crooked Man chase. The Silver Tiny Key is recovered after the chase.
- **Crimson Veil, approximately 75% and night hard mode:** trigger only when the recovered Gondian power source is charged in the Aqueduct. Nightfall by itself does not trigger it. The charge depletes stored energy that had helped the house suppress an Abyssal breach, allowing Voss's ritual and the breach to surge.

These are checkpoint milestones, not real-time timers or mechanically computed completion percentages.

### Early estate and Sune chain

1. With the pond full, take Oars from Donovan's Shack and reach the older sunken rowboat to retrieve the Ornate Mirror. An Old Fishing Rod at the pond landing supports optional fishing.
2. Fit the mirror to the mother's Art Studio vanity. The drawers yield **Brass Tiny Key #1** (grandfather-clock glass face/hour hand) and the Carriage Key.
3. The Carriage Key opens the family carriage, where the mother's remains and Ladder Hook are found. Use the Hook to lower the attic ladder. Attic contents include the Minute Hand, Clockwork Winder, and a storybook; a different storybook is in the roof chimney flue.
4. Use the Winder on four Sune busts in the order **SW → NE → NW → SE**. Their sequence reveals the concealed stone sliding relief. Exact order clue and sliding puzzle content remain Regionwright design work. The stash contains 1,000 gp; the Brass Key is accidentally concealed beneath the bag, not deliberately labeled as a Study key.
5. The Brass Key opens the Study. The Study note `587` leads to the Sluice Crank in Donovan's Shack lockbox. The Study also contains Rufus, the taxidermied dog, missing both eyes. The older “Tiny Key in the Study basket” placement is superseded by the Art Studio placement.

### Clock, pond, basement, and Aqueduct

- Brass Tiny Key #1 opens the grandfather clock face; its oversized hour hand combines with the attic Minute Hand at the roof chimney clock. Set noon and press the central button to reveal a Replacement Gear for the Aqueduct gate.
- Code `587` opens the shack lockbox for the Sluice Crank. Drain the pond to expose the submerged stairs and Weathered Key, which opens the basement. The Workshop yields a repair tool; use it on the parents' broken nightstand drawer to obtain the Aqueduct Gate Handle. Replacement Gear plus Handle opens the Aqueduct gate.
- The Bathhouse bathing pool also drains and yields a Screwdriver. This supports the parents' drawer repair path as specified in the latest puzzle audit. Preserve player alternatives where reasonable; some bypass policy and exact gate behavior still need design.
- Aqueduct backtracking must remain possible. The forest well allows reentry when the pond is full. The Waterworks Utility Room lies beyond a secondary Aqueduct door. With the sluice closed, pressure distribution can operate. Ordinary stones, dirt, and debris choke the Greenhouse sprinkler line; players may clear it with suitable tools or by hand using an appropriate Athletics, Sleight of Hand, or Acrobatics check. Regionwright owns the exact pressure interaction.
- Restoring the line supplies Greenhouse mixing water and opens an inner utility compartment with the father's journal warning Sophia against the Memorial Clearing at night and Rufus's Collar (psychic immunity while worn). Whether the Memorial Clearing is the same place as Sune's Grove is unresolved; do not equate them silently.

### Late-game Rufus and Crimson Veil chains

- A later return to the NW Sune bust triggers the second chase. Silver Tiny Key #2 opens the parents' Hope Chest, which holds an Ornate Music Box. Opening it reveals Prayer of Protection; using it at the mother's Art Studio Sune shrine opens the adjoining Candle Workshop, where the Wick is found.
- Combine the Wick with oil from Donovan's Shack at the Forgotten Gazebo lantern. The brief ignition clears cobwebs and reveals Rufus's second eye. The first eye is sewn into Sophia's torn stuffed bear (Old Iron Key from the parents' coat opens her room). Return both eyes to Rufus in the Study; his jaw reveals a button and his body reveals the **uncharged Gondian power source**.
- Return to the observed Aqueduct charger and charge the source. This is intentionally simple: no extra puzzle, repair, activation step, or special item. Charging triggers the Crimson Veil. The night sky becomes dark crimson, fiendish red fog covers the estate, music turns ominous, demons intensify, and the house becomes more desperate. Exact multi-scene implementation is not verified.

### Crimson Veil branches and final objective

- At Crimson Veil onset, the sixth **Miss Lulubug's Wondrous Tales** volume appears on Sophia's bed. The other sites are two books already on her shelf, the pond hollow log, attic, and chimney flue. Arrange all six in publication order on the Spine Mosaic; this yields Gond's half of the circular mechanism. Titles are retained in the chat and source artifacts; publication order/clues remain unproven.
- The Outhouse opens during the Veil. Copper Tiny Key #3 can be won with a parody Regionwright: Hunting & Gathering fishing minigame or retrieved cleverly by another method. It opens the Bathhouse soap-room lye case. Use lye with restored Greenhouse water and the established recipe/ingredients to make the fictional plant killer; clear vegetation at the far dock and reach the Boathouse. The Boathouse holds Prayer of Devotion (not the sixth book); use it at the Sune shrine to acquire Sune's half.
- Four final requirements are basement access, charged Gondian power, Gond's emblem half, and Sune's emblem half. Join the two halves as a circular Gond-and-Sune arm-in-arm mechanical actuator to open the final vault and retrieve Sune's Scrying Bowl.
- Great Room lockbox `027` contains an early water-filled, teardrop Dust of Dryness pellet; the Art Studio painting supplies its code clue. Return the bowl to Sune's statue hands and use the pellet so measured water flows through engineered channels and exposes the Undercroft entrance.
- Maldrath Voss's final ritual is beneath the memorial area/Undercroft. Vharzagul, a yugoloth rather than a demon, is intended to betray and kill Voss. Exact ritual interruption conditions, combat beats, and the cosmological link between the yugoloth summoning and Abyssal breach remain unresolved. The haunts predate the ritual and survive its interruption.
