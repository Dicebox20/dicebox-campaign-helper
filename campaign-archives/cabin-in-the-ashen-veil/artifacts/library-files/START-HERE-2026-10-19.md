## Setting, family, and haunts
- Neverwinter Wood, c. 1501 DR, roughly fifty years after Ash Zombies attacked the homestead around 1451 DR. Family father: highly skilled stonemason and tinkerer, admirer of **Gond**; built house from the ground up and constructed all its masonry and mechanisms. Mother: red-haired half-elf cleric of **Sune Firehair** and painter, created family paintings. Sophia blinded after severe childhood eye infection; younger unborn brother lost in miscarriage.
- Father built Sune's Grove as a secluded woodland sanctuary for mother, *not a cemetery*: **no graves/headstones**. Carefully crafted masonry lives harmoniously with trees, roots, vines and foliage; additional growth from fifty years abandoned. Walking ring at central statue, dirt trails to **four outer stone resting areas**, forest around them. User sketch defines layout; first generated grove image approved as rough aesthetic reference; later five images rejected. Splitting into center plus four corner/outer maps was discussed as concept only, not approved scene geometry.
- Five manifestations: Crooked Man = groundskeeper Donovan; Eyeless Girl = Sophia as fear-shaped ghost (dark/black imagined hair and void eye sockets not literal former disfigurement); Shadow That Sees = mother, wall/ground feminine shadow and appearing eye; Null Child = incomplete consciousness of miscarried unborn brother projected onto victim, with cosmic-horror mouth as feature, not metaphoric truth; House That Loves = father's protective *will* infused in house, communicates through imagery/intention, **not spoken exposition**. No sixth haunt; family's cat continues to roam and purr.
- In Ash Zombie assault parents hid Sophia in secured **Undercroft**; Donovan was trapped with her, both died there, leaving remains and signs of attempted escape. Parent death during attack. Summoner Maldrath Voss later attempts to bring **Vharzagul, a yagnoloth/yugoloth (not demon)** into mortal world in final undercroft ritual site; it kills Voss. Separate Abyssal/demonic intrusion and house crushing emerging demon have been discussed; exact planar reconciliation unfinished. Haunts predate ritual and survive stopping it. Family story inferred, never spoon-fed. Closing vision shows lost brother as ordinary newborn.
- Sune's Scrying Bowl was mother's spell focus for Scrying and painting; she removed it from shrine in her lifetime. Bowl later hidden in father's secured machinery during assault. Very late, return bowl to Sune statue's hands, then insert distinct water-filled teardrop Dust of Dryness pellet obtained early from Great Room lockbox **027**: measured water flows through engineered channels, opening Undercroft. NO Chauntea or graves. Bowl must not be needed to retrieve itself.
