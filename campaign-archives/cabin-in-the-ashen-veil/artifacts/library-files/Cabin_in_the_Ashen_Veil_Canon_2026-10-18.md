# Cabin in the Ashen Veil — Consolidated Established Canon

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: user-established creative design baseline and unresolved design ledger; **not** evidence of repository/KB synchronization or Foundry runtime implementation. This supersedes conflicting earlier *working campaign descriptions* (especially Chauntea devotion and the 29-map registry), but does not independently replace repository governance.

## Governing design principles
- User decides canon; proposals remain proposals until accepted. Build location registry from actual puzzle/story locations, not the original 29-map list. Scenes are not synonymous with locations.
- Halloween one-shot in Forgotten Realms, deep Neverwinter Wood, ~1501 DR. Family tragedy at Ash Zombie attack ~1451 DR. Interconnected Resident Evil-like exploration, puzzle chains and backtracking.
- Psychological horror. No blood or gore before the summoning chambers; apparent dismemberment at doghouse concerns stuffed toys and stuffing only.
- The House That Loves communicates **intent**, via crushing sensation and fragments of memories, never words, explicit supernatural emotion labels, or explanations; show hints, never admissions.

## Family and tragedy
- Father: human, black hair, brilliant stonemason/tinkerer/inventor, quiet follower of Gond; built house with intense protective devotion. The House is his protective will imprinted in architecture through necromantic/Netherese disturbances, not a separate father ghost.
- Mother: half-elf raised in elven culture, fiery red hair, painter, devoted cleric of **Sune Firehair** (supersedes Chauntea). Shrine to Sune prominent; private studio and worship area. Used Sune's Scrying Bowl as an ordinary spell focus for **Scrying** while painting, watching Sophia. Said: "Your mother has a special eye in every room to keep you safe." This underpins Shadow That Sees. Bowl absent from shrine because mother removed it for use; it was later deliberately secured during attack.
- Sophia: daughter, brown hair in life; lost vision after serious childhood eye infection. Spectral appearance has black hair and void eye sockets representing her *imagined* physical disfigurement, not literal historical look. Wants to see again.
- Unborn younger son: lost to miscarriage; manifested as Null Child. Last departure vision includes mother holding living newborn baby boy (no blood). Grief also intensified protectiveness.
- Donovan: kind, paternal groundskeeper, deeply loved by children, becomes Crooked Man. Rufus: family's dog, taxidermied after death; his missing eye is a button eye borrowed by Sophia for torn stuffed bear.
- During Ash Zombie assault parents concealed Sophia in Undercroft and hid the means of reopening it in father's most secure engineered contrivance; parents never returned. Sophia's and Donovan's bones lie within Undercroft; Donovan tried to dig/break through massive stone door but failed. Exact sequence of his entrance, timing of deaths, and whether physical key beyond bowl existed not fixed. Father's engineering masterpiece became tragic barrier.

## Haunts and second force
- Crooked Man (Donovan): tall contorted pale blue-gray figure, elongated neck/limbs, unsettling grin; protective pursuit; each distinct chase captures *at most one* character and deposits them in locked Sophia bedroom; later distinct chase may capture another. First bedroom key should become obtainable soon after first chase. House opposes forced/picked locks.
- Eyeless Girl (Sophia): ghost child with imagined empty eye sockets and black hair.
- Shadow That Sees (mother): elegant feminine surface-cast shadow, no visible caster; concealed bloodshot eye appears on discovery. Hidden Foundry Tile per eligible map, clicking reveals briefly opened eye, disappearance, persistent small puzzle clue; implementation proposed/issue #20, not certified.
- Null Child (unborn boy): distorted mummy-like infant; mouth reveals impossible truth; attempts communication, not purposeless malice.
- House That Loves (father's protective intent): enforces house rules, disapproval can be overwhelming, sometimes shifts architecture. Late encounter lesser demon emerging from Abyssal intrusion is physically crushed and forced through rift by house; players see broken protective memories, not explanatory words. Final battle house transmits protective memories (father with axe defending Sophia from bear; Donovan raising children from harm; father rescuing mother from water; father and Donovan against ash zombies). Buff called `The House That Loves` and yagnoloth debuff planned but numerical/API details undecided. House dormant rather than killed after finale. Family window departure tableau planned.
- Maldrath Voss, former servant of Szass Tam, works summoning in Undercroft, producing distinct hostile Abyssal-style phenomena; Vharzagul a **yagnoloth/yugoloth**, not an Abyss demon, turns on and kills summoner; final party showdown. How Abyssal incursion connects to yugoloth ritual is undecided.

## Grounds, maze, underground
- Roof/chimney: elaborate father's stone chimney clock; BOTH hands missing initially, correct setting and reward unknown. **Hour hand is in foyer grandfather clock**; minute hand location unknown. Father journal text: "Now that there's a clock on the chimney, Sophia will always know when it's time to come inside".
- Rufus doghouse: outside, shredded stuffed toys and stuffing, no real gore.
- Pond: deep with sluice gate missing removable crank; when opened, pond drains slowly via small drain, exposing submerged stairs. Hollow log near pond conceals book *Felix Fox and the Forgotten Kingdom*; father journal hints Sophia sheltered book from storm while reading by pond. Bottom stair contains two distinct leads: an unassigned pickup item for unrelated puzzle, and heavy waterproof masonry door opened with assembled two-part key.
- Engineered aqueduct: beyond masonry door; Crooked Man chase; escape via separate forest well, not necessarily original estate well/pump.
- Haunted Woodland Maze: forest well exit delivers characters at far end; traverse maze **in reverse** to wrought-iron estate-side gate; gate locked from estate side but can be unlocked from inside maze, bypassing/forcing forbidden by house's overwhelming intent.
- Forgotten Gazebo: old family picnic place within maze; father's stonework intact; rotted wooden canopy. **Standalone stationary puzzle and reward NOT YET DESIGNED**.
- Sune's woodland shrine (formerly E06 Memorial Clearing): Sune statue with missing bowl in hands; restore bowl late, reveals teardrop pellet receptacle, inserting early Dust of Dryness pellet discharges water safely into internal watertight chamber and measured narrow aqueducts to unlock sealed **Undercroft** access. Original Chauntea statue artwork obsolete; redraw later. No explicit instruction should reveal pellet fit.
- Privy: exterior outhouse. Donovan's Shack: separate residence on estate grounds, exact position unknown.
- Undercroft: Sophia/Donovan final refuge and remains, stone door excavation signs; Maldrath Voss's summoning chamber within. Earlier `B06 concealed Thayan lab` registry entry not reconciled with this location; not authoritative map count.
