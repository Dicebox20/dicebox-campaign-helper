**Item #:** SCP-XXXX

**Object Class:** Euclid (provisional)

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-XXXX is not to be deliberately elicited. Personnel who report a second awareness intruding on their thoughts or perceive the child-like figure are to be removed from the suspected exposure setting and given a quiet, medically supervised observation period. Interviews must use neutral prompts and stop if distress or confusion escalates. Until the conditions and consequences of projection are understood, no controlled exposure or attempt at direct communication is authorized.

**Description:** SCP-XXXX is believed to be the incomplete consciousness of a fetus lost through miscarriage. It manifests by projecting itself onto a living person, designated SCP-XXXX-1. The projection is fragmentary and pre-verbal; it has not demonstrated a stable identity or the ability to communicate through language. SCP-XXXX does not appear to understand the boundary between itself and the person onto whom it is projected.

Affected subjects describe the intrusion as a presence within their own awareness rather than an external voice. The experience takes on a cosmic-horror scale: an awareness that feels vast and impossible to contextualize presses into a developed mind, but does not convey a coherent message or hidden knowledge.

When a figure is perceived, it resembles a small, upright, desiccated fetal-infant form with leathery skin and an enormous open black mouth. The mouth is a visual feature of the projection; no separate effect or communication has been attributed to it. It is unknown whether this form reflects SCP-XXXX’s self-image or the subject’s way of perceiving it. No physical remains or fixed point of origin have been identified.

**Addendum XXXX.1: Associated Nursery**

At [REDACTED LOCATION], investigators found an unused nursery, pristine beneath a layer of dust. Its furnishings appeared to have been prepared long ago and left undisturbed. The room has yielded no evidence identifying the intended child or establishing a direct causal link to SCP-XXXX. It remains associated with the ongoing investigation.

**Addendum XXXX.2: Interview Excerpt**

**Interviewer:** Is it speaking to you?

**SCP-XXXX-1:** No.

**Interviewer:** What is it doing?

**SCP-XXXX-1:** I think it’s trying to know where it ends.

**Interviewer:** Does the mouth do anything?

**SCP-XXXX-1:** No. It just looks like that.
