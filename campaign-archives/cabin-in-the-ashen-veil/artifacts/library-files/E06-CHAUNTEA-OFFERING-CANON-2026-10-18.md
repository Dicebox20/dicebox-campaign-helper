# Cabin in the Ashen Veil — E06 Chauntea Offering Puzzle (Accepted Canon)

**Accepted:** 2026-10-18 by explicit user instruction, "lock this puzzle in as canon. The bowl will be one of the very last objects acquired."

## Approved progression

1. The party acquires a **distinctively shaped, water-filled Dust of Dryness pellet** early in the adventure, far before its purpose becomes apparent. Its location, shape, and acquisition puzzle are not yet decided.
2. The pellet can remain in inventory throughout much of the campaign; its function is not disclosed with acquisition.
3. The **stone offering bowl is one of the very last puzzle objects acquired**. Its location and acquisition puzzle are not yet decided.
4. The players take the bowl to **E06 — Old Memorial Clearing**, where the approved **kneeling statue of Chauntea** offers her hands toward a person in need.
5. Placing the bowl in the statue's hands reveals a **small concealed receptacle** in the bowl. The opening's outline precisely matches the pellet's distinctive shape, providing an epiphany through recognition rather than an explicit instruction.
6. Inserting the water-filled pellet releases stored water into a **small watertight internal chamber in the bowl**, which contains the magical discharge and meters water slowly through a small outlet.
7. The **tilted bowl** feeds a system of **small stone aqueducts**. Water activates the mechanism that **opens access to the lower chamber**.

## Rules for implementing the puzzle

- Preserve delayed-use item discovery, long-term inventory retention, and visual shape recognition as necessary narrative design constraints.
- The bowl must be obtained late; do not move it earlier to simplify the network.
- No explicit hint should tell players to combine the pellet and bowl; the receptacle geometry conveys the connection.
- The final E06 entrance is beneath the memorial clearing. Precise site architecture (mausoleum / grave / crypt) remains undecided unless subsequently accepted.
- This is the approved logical sequence, **not** evidence of Foundry implementation, an approved scene, or runtime verification.
- Foundry item use, effects, aqueduct animations/Regions, permissions, idempotency, failure recovery, and verification remain technical work.
