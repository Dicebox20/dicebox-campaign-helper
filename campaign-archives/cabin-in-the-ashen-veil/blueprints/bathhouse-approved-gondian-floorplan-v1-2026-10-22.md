## Lower floor, established puzzle canon

The **Rosewater Reliquary** remains a **separate lower-level Foundry destination beneath Main Bath**. It is *not drawn on this surface-floor reference sheet* and must be surveyed as its own subfloor plan.

- **Open sluice → drain pond and Main Bath** together. Screwdriver appears on exposed Main Bath bottom; three concentric rings can be solved **outer 90°, middle 270°, inner 180°**, unlocking floor hatch/stairs to lower Reliquary.
- **Scrying Bowl** recovered independently on the lower plinth; **not** in Allen's Secured Vault.
- An **inside-releasable dry service passage from Reliquary to Boiler Room** prevents softlocks and remains navigable when surface bath refills.
- **Waterworks restored + boiler solved + sluice closed + bath full + bath heated** is the existing five-condition recovery requirement; only Main Bath carries the explicit **4d8+8 HP, once per PC per long-rest cycle** benefit.
- **Herbal Room contains late keyed lye**, replacing the previously labelled Soap Room for House/Bathhouse v2 architecture; other puzzle canon and item quantities preserved.
