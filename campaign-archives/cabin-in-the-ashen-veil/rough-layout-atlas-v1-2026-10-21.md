# Cabin in the Ashen Veil — Rough Layout Atlas v1 (31 logical questline locations)

**Recorded:** 2026-10-21. **Status:** Conceptual *rough layouts*, owner requested before the unified house blueprint. **Not** approved metric room geometry, Map Creation PREPARED state, rendered map, Foundry Scene registry, or in-world implementation. The historical fixed 29-map roster is discarded. Stage variants (Sune dormant/active, Bath full/drained/hot, Pond full/drained, Crimson Veil overlays) are **not separate logical locations**.

## Drawing convention

All mini-layouts read **north at the top**; north/middle/south rows list important **zones**, not 5-ft grid cells. The vertical bars separate **west / center / east**, and are **not measured walls**. Doors, stairs and environmental relations listed below are **candidates** unless already marked owner-established in the decision catalogue. Do not project Walls, door IDs or hit regions from these sketches. All room-specific physical positions are proposed for spatial reconciliation; the approved **Carriage Yard exterior sketch and southwest Bathhouse fork** control the macro-layout.

**Hard continuity locks:** One independent Foundry scene for each real separately traversable house room; Bathhouse bathing/soap/boiler/Reliquary as distinct rooms; early item gates are not moved; Sune bowl mid-game from Rosewater Reliquary; vault reward Keystone; hidden Undercroft refuge shows Donovan/Sophia skeletons and inner door scratches after Keystone. Preserve story-bearing items at readable overhead scale; stage-only movable items are independent assets.
