## 4. Setting, family, and emotional foundation

- **Allen** (new owner-approved name, 2026-10-20): father, human, devotee of **Gond**, a **professional mason and tinkerer**. - **Sophia:** daughter, blind after a severe childhood illness; living form with brown hair (mix of Allen's black and Dahlia's red); supernatural ghost imagines long black hair and void-black eyes. The exact causal mechanism linking her illness, the pond incident, and sight loss remains open. Her room uses Old Iron Key and includes a torn bear and Spine Mosaic.
- **The Null Child:** the miscarried unborn son, incomplete consciousness projected in cosmic-horror form. He was **never a living newborn**; normal newborn appearance is for a supernatural closing family vision (chat #0484), and Sophia recognizes him as family after death. One child's grave exists in the Sune memorial area (chat #0875 and later canon); earlier “no graves” is superseded.

- **The House That Loves:** Allen's protective will, supernatural force associated with the Dread Ring and the Ruining. **Not** a speaking separate ghost. It can overwhelm minds and act violently to shelter its family; emotional communication through images and intent, not labeled exposition or explanatory dialogue.
- **Five sentient haunts total:** The Crooked Man (Donovan), Eyeless Girl (Sophia), Shadow That Sees (Dahlia), Null Child, House That Loves (Allen's will). Do not add a sixth.
- Timeline: **Allen dies before Dahlia** during the Ash Zombie attack. Dahlia unsuccessfully attempts to heal him, later dies after sheltering inside locked family carriage (chat #1131–1136, #1316). Donovan and Sophia's remains and failed escape evidence are in Undercroft; precise moment-by-moment geography needs reconciliation.

The owner edited the Rufus entry's closing line to: **“I am again reminded how incomplete I am without her.”** (chat #1314). The claim “dismantled mechanisms” in that draft requires consistency with in-game Rufus machine; do not silently certify the full prose.
