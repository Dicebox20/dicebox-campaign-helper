**Map policy:** Individual full rooms get individual Foundry scenes. The `Undercroft Approach` and `Donovan & Sophia's Refuge` are distinct physical spaces divided by the Keystone door and should be distinct maps if each is independently explored, as directed; `Ritual Chamber` is a further distinct destination. Whether the connecting hall merits its own map depends on its actual walkable extent and play purpose. The deprecated historical 29-scene roster must never serve as a target. The previous *pre-change* 28 questline estimate included only a single Undercroft scene, but these separately explorable areas likely add maps, as does the new Bowl standalone map. Update count through inventory, **not** quota.

**Mandatory visual fixtures per physical zone:**

| Zone | Static visual anchors | Stateful behavior / dependency |

Keep the environment pre-summoning largely psychological rather than overt gore according to earlier tone decisions.

## 3. Mid-game independent Bowl map — Bathhouse proposal

**Owner's intent:** A **standalone** navigable map for the Scrying Bowl around mid-game, plausibly exposed by draining the Bathhouse. It should not require completion of Allen's secured Vault, obtainment of Keystone, the Gond/Sune emblem halves, or Crimson Veil. The established Sluice Crank can cause a single mid-game *drain* affecting pond and Bathhouse pool.

**Proposed candidate (NOT canonical placement):** `Bathhouse — Lower Service Cistern / Forgotten Reliquary`, with discreet stone access that becomes physically reachable only when the Bathhouse bathing pool is empty. Its distinct scene would be a separate questline map under the individual-room map rule. Room architecture should support a credible hidden object that had been intentionally kept safe during the family's attack, consistent with the previously established rationale for hiding the Bowl.

| Feature | Starting state | Drain-state proposal | Trigger / result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bathhouse bathing pool | Full, Screwdriver obscured, possible hidden access submerged | Empty pool reveals Screwdriver and **separate concealed opening/steps** | Same Sluice Crank event; Bowl area may be entered when empty |
| Bowl-side concealed route | Not traversable while full | Passage revealed in emptied Bathhouse | Player deliberately enters a **separate Foundry scene**, not an auto-loot Region |
| Bowl display/support | Bowl not yet obtained | Visibly recoverable in the lower room | Player intentionally searches/interacts, obtains **Scrying Bowl** once |
| Bathhouse refilled state | Sluice closed after exploration | Pool filled again; concealed opening may be submerged | Must not strand characters or discard Bowl; escape/return policy remains design gate |

**Anti-softlock:** If the only access is via emptied pool, the party must be able to leave and return before refilling, and the refill operation must not occur while occupied without a safety policy (refill interlock, persistent alternate egress, or GM/engine rescue, to be chosen later). The new Bowl quest should never depend on the boiler puzzle or healed bathing, since those occur after refilling and Waterworks restoration. Players must be able to recover Bowl during the mid-game drained state even if the Aqueduct first pressure puzzle has not yet been solved.

## 4. Healing Bathhouse — exact conditions

### Canonical eligibility gate
```text
HEALING USABLE
  ONLY WHEN:
  sluice CLOSED  (drain disabled)
  AND Bathhouse pool FILLED (physical water-level confirmed)
  AND main Aqueduct Waterworks puzzle RESTORED
  AND Bathhouse Boiler Room pressure/heating puzzle COMPLETE
  AND bath water HEATED/operational (physical functional state)
```

The owner now explicitly mandated the first **two** conditions. The others were previously approved (Boiler cannot operate until Waterworks restores the Boiler supply line; functioning boiler heats baths and activates healing). A local hot pipe alone is **not** healing eligibility. The bath level must be derived from the same source-of-truth water-system state as the sluice, not from an out-of-sync visual overlay. If sluice later reopens or bathing pool empties, **healing disables** even if the boiler has previously been repaired; whether boiler heat persists as a mechanical state is a later configuration detail.

### Example player-facing interactions (proposals, not approved balancing rules)

- **Pool enter/interact**: a deliberate `Rest in heated bath` action may activate healing only when all five conditions hold, avoiding auto-healing on accidental crossings.
- **Conditions absent**: provide physical feedback (cold water / empty pool / unpressurized boiler) rather than nonsensical healing rewards.
- **Recovery cap, amount, frequency, eligible PCs, healing duration, safety in Crimson Veil, whether short/long rest counts:** **OPEN**. No healing value, cooldown or D&D rest classification assigned.
- A full pool **does not mean** the standalone Bowl room behind a submerged access point stays available; that location is separately accessed only during appropriate drained state unless an alternate owner-approved route exists.
