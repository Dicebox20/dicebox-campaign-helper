# Boathouse: buried keepsake
Owner decision, 2026-10-23: the next EXT-07 Boathouse area includes a patch of earth which players can dig to find Sophie's Favorite Doll. The owner explicitly specifies the item gives **+1 to saving throws** and **+1 to attack rolls**. Sophia is the established character; Sophie is the owner's affectionate name. The doll is an optional separate wondrous accessory, not the stuffed bear containing the Rufus eye.

EXT-07 architecture: distinct scene reached by bidirectional teleport from the **east side of the Pond EXT-02**. West border houses the safe arrival/return landing; one forest trail goes east to the Boathouse. An obstructing magical-thorn patch along the internal trail remains governed by the established late Greenhouse Plant-Killer solution; Prayer of Devotion stays separately recoverable inside Boathouse. A small disturbed soil patch by a root/stone off the trail can be investigated, dug, then claimed once; the hidden doll does not appear in the permanent background. Place soil beyond the growth gate to make this a late optional reward. Keep the arrival accessible and the return safe before barrier clearance.

Item design: Sophie's Favorite Doll, optional Wondrous Item accessory, proposed rare and requiring attunement (only the bonus values are explicit owner authority). While worn and attuned, grants **+1 to all attack rolls** (melee, ranged and spells) and **+1 to all saving throws**, not damage, AC or checks. The source is unique; explicit separate Dig and Pick-Up actions, never automatic Region-entry loot. Discovery DC 12 Perception or Investigation is a designer suggestion only, and deliberate digging can always recover it; no story lock is contingent on finding it. The item's bonuses must be implemented and verified for dnd5e, not merely written into item description.

Stage art should depict aged boathouse, wooded landing, path, subtly disturbed soil and thorn-covered choke point with removable Tile/Region visuals. Low-key late-autumn neutral illumination. The west edge is the only scene crossing, to EXT-02. Art render, inspection, item creation and Foundry build remain separate workflow gates.
