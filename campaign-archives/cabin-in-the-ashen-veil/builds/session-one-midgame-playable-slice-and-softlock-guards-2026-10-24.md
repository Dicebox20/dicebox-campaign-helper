## No-softlock rules
1. Every required clue remains discoverable without a mandatory Perception roll; give clear **inspect** action from physically recognizable fixture. Failure costs time or feedback, never erases sole key or prevents retrial.
2. Unique items persist as logical state: unclaimed fixture, carried, installed, or recovered. No duplicate key from scene re-entry; no grant just for entering a Region. Never consume a required item before both write and verified outcome complete.
3. GM-authoritative RegionBehavior interaction controls and Journal read/actions; ordinary players can use them without permission to execute world Macros. Player-private introspection/hints appear over their token rather than public chat; explicit interaction chat cards are permitted.
4. Stairways, Study exterior, roof window, attic hatch, aqueduct gate, Pond/Bath stages and well transitions require bidirectional, in-bounds tested token landings, with a single read-only routing matrix and native canary for each.
5. Any puzzle UI unavailable at launch has a **guarded GM-recovery action**, not a visible arbitrary bypass. The 3×3 Sune relief can be reconciled once with exactly one set of rewards. Recovered item/puzzle flag should preserve chronology and log reason.
6. Protect party from half-built areas: implement **narratively normal closed/inactive mechanisms only where canonical**, show feedback that discovery is possible but operation currently unresolved, and keep a safe route back to released content. Do not activate the Crimson Veil automatically or let an unfinished late trigger strand PCs. A planned GM story pause after the Aqueduct chase is preferable to a hard invisible wall.
7. End-to-end preflight with non-GM test player on a cloned/canary state: complete all required interactions from arrival through first chase, repeat actions, wrong combinations, scene reloads, reconnects, absent item, split party, unsafe water stage, captured PC, GM emergency release and Well return. Trace state and UUID receipts.
