## Reconciliation and contrary interpretations

The independent official U.S. litigation record and Canadian registration data converge on **Space Exploration Technologies Corp.**, rather than demonstrating that a single separately owned Starlink subsidiary holds the core word-mark portfolio. However, USPTO litigants may be named as owners/claimants and filings may be historical. The live Trademark Status and Document Retrieval (TSDR) owner/status/maintenance histories and native USPTO Assignment Center instrument for each specific registration are still required before a certified current U.S. owner conclusion. The Delaware-to-Texas entity conversion lead should not be treated as a commercial asset transfer into Starlink.

A trademark is a jurisdiction- and goods/services-bounded intangible right. It cannot establish the titled owner of satellites, FCC rights, launch facilities, patents, hardware, software source code, or subscriber contracts. Any hypothetical commercial-Starlink-only carve-out would require identifying transferred marks plus associated goodwill, any retained cross-business licences and coexistence restrictions, global registrations, litigation and recordal/consent steps. Starshield and other SpaceX business uses cannot be assumed to disappear or become assets of the acquisition.
