## Key distinction established

The EchoStar/SpaceX spectrum deal must not be modeled as an already completed conveyance of all licenses directly to SpaceX. SpaceX's June 30, 2026 Form 10-Q and EchoStar's May 22, 2026 Form 8-K describe two distinct closing steps: **(1) transfer from EchoStar to Spectrum Business Trust 2025-1 on May 22, 2026, completed**, and **(2) later transfer from that trust to SpaceX, the Spectrum Acquisition Closing, targeted November 30, 2027**, contingent on conditions. FCC consent was granted May 12, 2026. These are date-specific reported facts; no later source-native instrument establishing an accelerated final closing was recovered in this pass. An FCC approval, trust-held license rights, SpaceX prepaid economic interests, and direct SpaceX title are legally different states.

The proposed consideration, approximately **$19.6 billion**, includes approximately $11.1 billion in SpaceX equity and up to $8.5 billion associated with designated EchoStar debt/cash adjustment. This is the reported transaction consideration, **not** the standalone fair value of commercial Starlink and not proof that spectrum is unencumbered or assignable to a hypothetical public buyer. SpaceX's June 2026 filing reports $856 million advanced to the trust by June 30 as prepaid assets, not acquired intangible spectrum license ownership. A separate **October 8, 2026 announced Grain Management 800 MHz spectrum agreement**, reported by Reuters as pending FCC approval, is a distinct transaction and cannot be combined with the EchoStar spectrum licenses, acquisition consideration or completed-closing status.
