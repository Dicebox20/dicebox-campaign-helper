## Scope and findings

1. **2020 BITS applicant and holder.** CRTC procedural letter 6 July 2020 records a 1 May 2020 registration letter and BITS request submitted by **Space Exploration Technologies Corp.** under docket **8190-S206-202002799**. CRTC approval letter **15 October 2020** names the same corporate entity, says its application was completed 15 May 2020, and approves the application with enclosed BITS licence. It expressly cautions that BITS licensing **does not itself authorize** facilities-based carrier or non-facilities-based service-provider operations; applicable separate frameworks, ownership rules and registrations remain necessary. Thus historical BITS holder identity is substantiated, but current live licence record and assignment history are not.
2. **Another Canadian-facing legal name appears in later proceedings.** CRTC Telecom Regulatory Policy **2025-9** names **SpaceX Canada Corp.** and describes that party's participation regarding potential wholesale services in the Far North. CRTC Telecom Order **2026-35** similarly names **SpaceX Canada Corp.** as a party relevant to a costs award. Neither demonstrates an executed BITS licence transfer from Space Exploration Technologies Corp., the corporate registration jurisdiction/number of SpaceX Canada Corp., or the asset ownership chain.
3. **Provider lists vs identity.** CRTC public accessibility plan, feedback and progress-report lists identify **Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (Starlink)**, corroborating regulated Canadian-facing service-organization identity, but these are accessibility compliance indexes rather than native BITS active-licence roster or corporate share register.
4. **CRTC/ISED separation.** CRTC's current registration guidance states telecommunications providers register with the CRTC and providers carrying international traffic require a BITS licence. Separate ISED guidance says foreign-licensed satellites require ISED foreign satellite approval to be used by Canadian earth stations. ISED's **21 April 2026** E-band decision names **SpaceX Canada Corp.** as requester in its title and temporarily authorizes frequencies for two identified Canadian gateway sites; this is spectrum authorization context, not CRTC licence or proof of gateway asset title.

## Contradictions, competing interpretations, and limitations

- Distinct names in primary government records might represent different service and spectrum entities, authorized agents, or reorganizations. The records cited here do **not** resolve their relationship, share ownership, licence assignments, or corporate successor status.
- A 2020 BITS approval is historical. Current validity, amendments, termination, assignment, and whether the enclosed licence conditions were subsequently satisfied need a native current CRTC registry extract and docket review.
- The accessibility lists are not substitutes for CRTC's provider/BITS licence-specific database.
- ISED approval to communicate with foreign satellites does not by itself convey title to satellites, gateways, spectrum rights, subscribers, or intellectual property.
- No original complete 36-topic/subcategory acceptance mapping was authenticated in this pass. Earlier research evidence is retained and unaffected.
