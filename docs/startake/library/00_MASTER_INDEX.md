# Starlink Communications Infrastructure: Moral and Public-Interest Research Topics

**Pre-drafting taxonomy · 19 primary topics · Proposed research structure**

One document per primary topic. A **subcategory is a constituent subject of the topic**, not a research task, question, conclusion, or evidence status. Source records and specific arguments will be placed under the appropriate subcategory when investigated.

**Scope:** Ethical assessment of concentrated private control of the Starlink communications network, including documented decisions attributed to Elon Musk or other relevant corporate/government actors. The proposed acquisition target remains **Starlink only**. SpaceX aerospace operations remain outside scope; Starshield is a dependency and national-security context.

**Purpose:** Assemble arguments and evidence concerning private control, public duties, acquisition, compensation, civil liberties, innovation, and risks associated with government ownership. This collection is **not** a finding that acquisition is ethically required, nor a claim of wrongdoing or personal medical causation.

## Taxonomy principles

1. **Topic** is the primary moral research domain and receives one document.

2. **Subcategory** is an inherent component or subject within that domain.

3. **Evidence** (records, studies, incidents, primary-source links and competing claims) belongs inside the appropriate subcategory.

4. **Legal analyses** remain in the separately maintained 17-topic legal collection, with cross-references rather than duplicate findings.

5. **Further subdivision** is reserved for subcategories containing multiple meaningfully distinct subjects.

6. **No personal medical inference:** Musk’s reported self-disclosure is a permissible research subject; claims that a diagnosis causes harmful, immoral, or reckless decisions require individual evidence that is not supplied by disclosure or group-level studies.

## Where a deeper second level may eventually help

- **M05.03 Service restrictions:** separate distinct incidents by date and product only once the chronology and responsible authority are verified.

- **M06.03 Privileged operational access:** later separate satellite-command, identity/access management, service authorization, and software deployment if independent technical evidence warrants it.

- **M11.01–M11.06 Public support:** potentially separate agencies and funding instruments after collecting transaction-level records.

- **M17.05 Civilian-military partition:** potentially separate ordinary broadband, disaster continuity, defense service, and classified systems after their operational boundaries are established.

## Research status

Organizational framework only. No empirical claims, judgments about individuals, or moral conclusions should be treated as verified merely because a subject appears in this index.
