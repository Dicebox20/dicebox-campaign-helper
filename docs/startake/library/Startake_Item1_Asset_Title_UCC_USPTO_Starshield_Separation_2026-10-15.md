## Audit and legal conclusions

1. **Separate four concepts:** legal owner, secured debtor/pledgor, licence holder, and economic/business segment. None substitutes for another. An individual shareholder or controlling person does not personally own the company's hardware by virtue of beneficial equity control.
2. **UCC search needed:** Entity-by-entity registered legal names, predecessors and jurisdictions (Texas, Delaware and applicable earlier formations, plus local fixture/real estate records). Preserve filing numbers, dates, collateral descriptions, secured parties, amendment chains, terminations and search certificates. Article 9 filings are claims of security interests, not proof of enforceability/attachment for each asset.
3. **Patent/IP search needed:** Exact-name USPTO assignee and assignor exports for Space Exploration Technologies Corp., Space Exploration Holdings LLC, SpaceX Services Inc. and other confirmed legal subsidiaries; reel/frame PDFs, patent families, current assignments, security-interest notices, and exclusive licensing terms. Trade secrets, copyright/source code and overseas patents cannot be completely inferred from US patent records.
4. **Creditor restrictions:** Retrieve all operative credit agreements, amendments, asset definitions, security agreements, Starlink dispositions exceptions and schedules; identify actual obligor, lien perfection, release mechanics and lender approval thresholds. A contractual disposition restriction is not necessarily an asset lien or a conclusion that a congressional acquisition is impossible.
5. **Starshield clean carve-out:** Distinguish excluded dedicated/classified Starshield assets from commercial Starlink assets and shared technologies; demand a *shared asset resolution schedule*: legal owner, usage, rights, classification, export/security constraints, transferability, license-back, continuity TSA and independent review.
6. **Transaction architecture:** Compare asset sale with stock/subsidiary separation, for each legal entity. Identify which assets could transfer, which must stay with retained SpaceX under licence/supply agreement, and which require regulatory, contractual, creditor or foreign permission. No definitive purchase perimeter can be certified from public financial filings alone.
