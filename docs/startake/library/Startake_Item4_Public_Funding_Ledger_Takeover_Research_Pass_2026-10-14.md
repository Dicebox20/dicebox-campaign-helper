## Executive conclusion

The primary-source record confirms that U.S. agencies **procure actual Starlink terminals and connectivity service**, frequently from `SPACE EXPLORATION TECHNOLOGIES CORP.` itself, rather than an independently titled Starlink company. Such payments are **procurement**, not automatically government *subsidies*. Agency procurement records separately show **obligation**, **outlay**, **current award**, and **potential ceiling** amounts, and sometimes show cancellations; those are **not interchangeable**. A major DISA/Space Force communications contract vehicle groups commercial connectivity, Starshield, and unspecified PLEO services, so its $475.86m **combined obligations** are **not** an auditable Starlink-only amount. The 2020 RDOF winning bid for $885.51m over ten years was **not authorized for financial assistance**, rather than an $885.51m realized subsidy. The SEC's Connectivity segment also reports enterprise/government and Starshield-linked earnings, so **segment revenue is not a ledger of public funds**.

## Required classification rules

1. Use legal recipient **exactly as reported** (e.g., Space Exploration Technologies Corp.), not a marketing name. Search parent and subsidiary/affiliate UEIs/CAGE identifiers and avoid attributing SpaceX-wide funding to Starlink without a service/satellite description and legitimate allocation basis.
2. Distinguish **procurement (goods/services delivered for public benefit)** from **financial assistance**, direct grants, loans, guarantees, research partnerships, tax benefits and in-kind/indirect support. Do not describe contract price as an unreciprocated subsidy.
3. Store separate columns for `announced`, `initial potential ceiling`, `current potential ceiling`, `gross positive actions`, `negative adjustments`, `net obligated`, `outlayed`, `revenue recognition`, and `actual cash receipt` (the last may be unavailable). Do not equate zero USAspending outlays to nonpayment without Treasury and agency data-quality checks.
4. **De-duplicate** `Prime award unique key + action/modification number`; identify IDV parents separately from each child award. Aggregate **net current** awards only after verifying child/prime relationship and cancellations; don't add parent IDV combined totals to children.
5. Classify service as `commercial Starlink direct / likely Starlink but confirm / mixed PLEO / Starshield excluded / launch & non-Starlink SpaceX excluded / future or denied assistance / unclear`. A recipient match alone is insufficient.
6. Keep federal, state, local and international funding distinct; cite granting authority and *actual* disbursement or benefit realization records; treat missing data as **unknown**, not zero.
