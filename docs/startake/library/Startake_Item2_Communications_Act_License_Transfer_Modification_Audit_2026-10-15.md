## Synthesis, opposing views and legal boundaries

**Transfer approval is a separate gate.** Acquiring shares or specified Starlink physical assets does not establish that the transferee may operate under all existing FCC authorizations immediately. The federal licence is a conditioned permission to use spectrum, not ownership of the electromagnetic spectrum; an assignable economic interest in a licence still must be analyzed alongside §310(d), Part 25, individual grant conditions, and the transaction form. Nothing in the reviewed text independently transfers foreign permissions, government contracts or classified assets.

**Competing interpretation:** A hypothetical future act of Congress could expressly establish special vesting, transfer, transition or federal operation arrangements. This pass does not evaluate any actual such act; it does not claim that §310(d) necessarily defeats a constitutionally valid, specific later statute. Conversely, without such legislation and authoritative regulator interpretation, the ordinary FCC process cannot be presumed bypassed. Section 316 modification and section 312 revocation are not equivalent to a general confiscation power. A licence holder may lack title to spectrum while retaining distinct compensable private interests, a question routed to the independent compensation analysis.
