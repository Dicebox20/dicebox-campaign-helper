## Narrow question and findings

Determine whether the **current CRTC provider-registry category** and precise licensed/registered legal identity can be authenticated for **SpaceX Canada Corp.** (formerly Tibro Canada Corp.) and **Space Exploration Technologies Corp.**, independently of their historic BITS applications and ISED satellite spectrum arrangements.

1. **Registration taxonomy is not a single permit.** CRTC's current official provider-registration guidance states that organizations offering telecommunications services must register; listed categories include resellers, resellers of high-speed retail internet, BITS licensees, competitive local-exchange carriers, non-dominant carriers and wireless carriers. A company may have more than one category. CRTC categorizes facilities-based providers as owners/operators of transmission facilities and non-facilities-based providers as those not owning/operating transmission facilities; international-traffic BITS licensing is separately applicable. Merely finding a BITS docket **does not establish current facilities-based carrier status or title to gateway assets**.
2. **Legal consequences of category differ.** Facilities-based providers have CRTC registration and annual Canadian ownership/control reporting duties and must meet relevant ownership regulations. Non-facilities-based providers are not subject to that ownership/control regime in the same fashion, though they have registration, consumer-safeguard and data-reporting duties. Therefore no carrier ownership/control compliance conclusion follows without the native registration category and carrier-specific filings.
3. **Original legal names remain distinct.** The CRTC's official 2019 BITS docket listing `8190-T230-201902883` identifies **SpaceX Canada Corp. (formerly TIBRO Canada Corp.)**, showing a historic BITS grant and name-change history with displayed period extending to 2029. The separate CRTC 2020 approval under `8190-S206-202002799` identifies **Space Exploration Technologies Corp.** These are separate dossiers, not proof of a transfer, and the displayed 2029 historic date is not certified present good standing.
4. **Current actual provider-category roster not verified.** CRTC's Facilities-Based, Non-Facilities-Based, and BITS responsibilities pages link to a native application-list endpoint at `applications.crtc.gc.ca/portail-portal/eng/listes-lists/registration/5`. Direct follow-through during this pass returned **502 Bad Gateway** for facilities/non-facilities/BITS list links. Therefore we did **not** obtain a native current per-entity provider-category, current registration number, registration effective date, compliance status, or exact registration name. CRTC accessibility-progress-report lists mentioning Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (Starlink) are **not** a substitute for the telecom-provider category register.
5. **CRTC applications require entity/title information rather than proving it.** Current CRTC registration instructions require applicant name, category, registration status, telecom services and a facilities diagram/owners for a facility-based applicant. They are rules for applications; **no SpaceX-specific submitted application, diagram or owners schedule** was recovered here.
