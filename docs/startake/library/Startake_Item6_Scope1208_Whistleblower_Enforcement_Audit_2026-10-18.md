## 2. Findings and adverse checks

### A. Government-corporation employees are not categorically unprotected

[5 U.S.C. §2302(a)(2)(A), (C)(i)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-121/t5/s2302/) expressly accommodates government corporations for the specified whistleblower-related prohibitions, including subsection (b)(8) and the listed portions of (b)(9). The exception is not wholesale application of all prohibited-personnel-practice rules. The [OSC's government-corporation guidance](https://www.osc.gov/services/outreach/2302cert-gov/) confirms limited coverage and related employee education obligations. **Application to this draft is an inference:** Bill §201's wholly owned corporation and chapter 91 treatment support this route, but the exact position, personnel action, protected activity and applicable exclusions still require an actual coverage determination.

[Section 1214(a)(3)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t5/s1214/) generally requires approaching the Office of Special Counsel before an individual Board action unless a direct Board appeal is independently available. [Section 1221(a), (c), (g) and (h)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t5/s1221/) supplies a qualifying individual corrective-action route, possible stays, specified relief and judicial review. This is not a blanket immediate district-court remedy. A workforce notice must identify the correct route and its requirements rather than merely promising “protected channels.”

### B. Contractor personnel use a different statutory and factual test

[41 U.S.C. §4712(a)–(c)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t41/s4712/) addresses specified employees of federal contractors and other covered recipients, eligible disclosures and recipients, an Inspector General complaint path, agency action and court access after statutory exhaustion. The statute also makes its rights nonwaivable, provides notice duties and preserves classified-information limits. A corporate supplier relationship, commercial service invoice or general federal funding reference does not itself prove that an individual and disclosure satisfy those tests.

For division B, [41 U.S.C. §133(4)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t41/s133/) includes a wholly owned Government corporation fully subject to chapter 91. **Inference:** this is a plausible basis for the proposed operator's covered procurement relationships; it is not a finding that every existing SpaceX contract transfers, every supplier becomes a federal contractor, or every contractor employee is covered. Actual award, employer, work and disclosure facts remain missing. The [OSC coverage page](https://www.osc.gov/services/prohibited-personnel-practices/whocanfile/) separately warns that ordinary federal-contractor employees do not obtain its employee corrective-action process merely through contractor status.

[FAR 3.900–3.906](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/subpart-3.9) implements the civilian contractor program with express DoD, NASA, Coast Guard and intelligence-related exceptions. The [52.203-17 clause text](https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/current/far/compiled_html/subpart_52.2.html) provides employee notice and subcontract flow-down language. Those sources support requiring applicable clauses and notices; they do not justify declaring one clause universally applicable to all inherited operating contracts. Specialized defense/intelligence routes were identified as exclusions and were not audited here.

### C. A new district-court shortcut should not be inferred

[Elgin v. Department of Treasury, 567 U.S. 1, 5–12 (June 11, 2012)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-567/pdf/USREPORTS-567-1.pdf) holds that the CSRA's review scheme supplies the exclusive judicial path for the covered employees and adverse actions at issue, including their constitutional statutory challenge. The result depends on coverage and the statutory scheme, rather than the mere label “constitutional claim.” The decision does not classify this proposed corporation's future workforce or decide every type of contractor claim.

The temporal/adverse check found [Margolin v. National Association of Immigration Judges, No. 25-767 (May 26, 2026), per curiam slip pp.1–5](https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/courtorders/052626zor_6j36.pdf). Its holding reverses a departure from party-presentation principles; it is not a new universal substantive decision on whistleblower coverage or remedies. The opinion recounts CSRA channeling, while Justice Thomas's further merits discussion (joined by Justice Barrett) is a concurrence. This distinction prevents treating the concurrence as the Court's holding. Recent LDH results were otherwise off-scope; no exhaustive negative citator result is claimed.

### D. The concrete bill gap is implementation specificity

**Draft-text finding:** Bill v1.11 §206(c) prohibits retaliation and promises channels, but specifies no coverage/remedies schedule or contractual implementation. Section 207 creates an express action for §§203–205, not §206(c); §501 separately provides an Act-wide nonmonetary immunity waiver subject to specific review routes. Therefore “no workforce remedy exists” would overstate this audit. The existing statute-specific remedies, APA questions and any residual Act claim must be assessed by the competent forum. This pass does not treat an immunity waiver as proof of jurisdiction, a cause of action or an exception to a specific review scheme. See the [employee corrective-action framework](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t5/s1221/) and [Elgin](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-567/pdf/USREPORTS-567-1.pdf).

**Drafting decision:** Add operationally checkable implementation duties in §206(c), preserving independently applicable legal coverage and procedures. Do not add a general workforce district-court action or amend §207/§501 in this bounded pass. This is a proposed legislative policy choice supported by the differentiated statutory architecture, not an assertion that current law requires this exact schedule. Any decision to create a broader supplemental cause of action needs a separately scoped forum, remedies and preclusion analysis.
