## Executive finding

Published Starlink Terms of Service distinguish purchased customer equipment, for which **title passes to the customer upon delivery**, from **rented kits that remain Starlink property**. The installed software is **licensed, not sold** to kit users. Customer hardware transfers, account/service assignment, kit rental conversion, and Starlink-side assignment are governed by separate conditions. Accordingly, a Starlink-only acquisition cannot simply list all installed customer terminals as Starlink-owned assets: the owner of a purchased kit may be the customer, while rented hardware may present a retained-equipment claim. Neither the online terms nor a model assumption proves exact kit inventory, contractual counterparty, title of a particular serial-numbered kit, assignable software rights, or customer continuity following acquisition.
