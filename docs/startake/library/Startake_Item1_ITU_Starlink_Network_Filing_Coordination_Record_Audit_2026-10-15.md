## Interpretation and counterevidence

- **Notifying administration ≠ national operator ≠ equity owner ≠ spacecraft/ground equipment title.** The official Norway/United States attribution is an important caution against assuming the ITU network right belongs to a standalone U.S. Starlink corporation. The ITU statement concerns responsibility under international radio regulations, not the private chain of title.
- **Submission ≠ coordination completion ≠ recorded assignment ≠ authorization in each country.** A BR filing can be an early procedural step. A precise network-level BR IFIC / MIFR extract and national operating permissions must be reconciled before asserting valid current international operation.
- **Counterevidence/qualification:** U.S. submissions to the RRB confirm the United States plays a role; this does not contradict the Board's more specific identification of Norway as the responsible administration for the discussed system. Do not generalize that Norway controls every network or owns any spacecraft.
- As-received entry `USA2026-82125` / LUNARSAT-1 was viewed as a demonstration that ITU publishes notice IDs and frequency tables, but it is **not a Starlink record and is excluded from the Starlink evidence count**.
