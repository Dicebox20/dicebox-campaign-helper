## Core findings — bounded to agency-indexed order text

1. **Order and applicant:** FCC Space Bureau `DA 26-36`, adopted/released **January 9, 2026**, concerns **Space Exploration Holdings, LLC**, callsigns **S2992/S3069**, with five ICFS applications `SAT-LOA-20200526-00055`, `SAT-AMD-20210818-00105`, `SAT-AMD-20221216-00175`, `SAT-AMD-20241017-00228`, `SAT-MOD-20241011-00224`. The published order says its applications were **granted in part and deferred in part, with conditions**. This is permission for specified operations by a named FCC licensee, *not proof of all spacecraft ownership*. [FCC1, FCC2]
2. **Numerical authorized-versus-proposed boundary:** FCC authorized a **second 7,500 Gen2 satellites**, taking authorized total to **15,000**; its order **deferred the remaining 14,988 proposed Gen2 satellites** from the requested **29,988**. None of these authorized quantities proves satellites were built, launched, operational, free of liens, or transferable. [FCC1, FCC2]
3. **Orbital limits:** order allows deployment flexibility across shells at **340, 345, 350, 355, 365, 480 and 485 km** with plane/spacecraft-per-plane parameters and ITU coordination requirements. It deferred satellites proposed **above 600 km**. The exact authorized orbital envelope and technical parameters remain subject to the issued conditional grant, updated schedules and subsequent amendments. [FCC2]
4. **Spectrum is conditional, not fungible property:** agency text describes limited additional frequencies, including direct-to-cell outside the United States in specified mobile bands, and waiver/beam-operation conditions. Other requested bands and outside-scope operations were deferred. Actual national authorizations, coordination and current rule status must be checked per geography, frequency and call sign. [FCC1, FCC2]
5. **Deployment milestones:** FCC agency indexed grant describes the continuing Gen2 service-constellation benchmark of **50 percent by December 1, 2028 and full deployment by December 1, 2031**, subject to bond, milestones, licensing limits and possible consequences for failure. These are *future compliance targets*, not current satellite counts or demonstrated milestone satisfaction. Distinct legacy V-band milestones should not be mixed into Gen2 totals. [FCC1, FCC3]
