## Counterevidence and limits

The most important contrary evidence to an asserted need for immediate public acquisition is that GAO found hybrid satellite solutions potentially preferable *in a studied DOD context*. Yet hybrid feasibility in that context says nothing definitive about rural commercial subscribers, disaster coverage, international licensing, or urgent capacity at equal price. The strongest acquisition case remains hypothetical: demonstrable inadequacy of competitive procurement, contract step-in, regulation and technical redundancy where public continuity requirements genuinely cannot be enforced. These opposing possibilities require a mission-specific test with independently sourced performance and risk measures.

**Legal correction:** Policy proportionality/least-intrusive assessment is a recommended public-decision requirement, **not a universal constitutional strict-necessity element** of every Fifth Amendment taking. Public-use legality, explicit congressional authority, property-owner compensation and economic prudence are independent matters. No legal opinion or proof of a valid acquisition mechanism was obtained.
