Treat specific notices as authoritative indexed source leads, but obtain native agency copies and current ICFS data before claiming audited real-time licence status. The Companies House register and SpaceX SEC 10-Q were directly identifiable; no proprietary data room was accessed. This is a bounded verification pass, **not a certified full current FCC fleet portfolio**.

## Asset and regulatory perimeter rules for future verification

1. **Corporate control ≠ asset title ≠ licensed operator ≠ operating segment.** Assemble evidence on separate rows, not one 'owned by Musk' field.
2. **Per authorization**, collect licensee legal name, FRN, ICFS application/file number, call sign, service, licensed location/orbits and bands, grant/expiration date, controlling parent, current disposition and transfer restrictions. Preserve temporary, expired, dismissed, conditional and fully granted states separately.
3. **Separate FCC consent analysis from economic acquisition.** A bill authorizing compulsory or negotiated acquisition does not by itself establish each FCC assignment, foreign license or contract novation.
4. **Do not fold Starshield or retained SpaceX launch/AI into Starlink solely from a financing agreement's definition or Connectivity accounting.** Request granular clean carve-out schedules.
5. **Do not assert a zero-or-unlimited ownership percentage:** Companies House PSC is a local control designation and current US corporate equity chain remains unverified.
