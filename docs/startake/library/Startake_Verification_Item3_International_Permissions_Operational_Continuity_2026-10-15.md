## Competing cases and remaining unknowns

**Feasibility hypothesis:** A hypothetical buyer could purchase genuinely dedicated Starlink assets, use operating company transition services and separately procure launch, manufacturing and NOC support. That is a deal-structure proposal, not observed transferability or tested resilience.

**Strongest contrary case:** Global authorizations may attach to distinct local operators, and regulators can impose ownership, national-security and service conditions. The lack of executed arm's-length launch, software, staff and manufacturing services creates substantial unknown operating and fiscal exposures. Segregation from Starshield is not established. A country-by-country consent effort can fail even with U.S. congressional authority.

**What not to assert:** Neither Ofcom's four variations nor ISED's general procedure guarantees host-state transfer consent. The ITU MIFR is not a conveyance of private property. The UNOOSA registry is not a shareholder ledger. The SpaceX segment is not the same as the proposed acquisition perimeter.
