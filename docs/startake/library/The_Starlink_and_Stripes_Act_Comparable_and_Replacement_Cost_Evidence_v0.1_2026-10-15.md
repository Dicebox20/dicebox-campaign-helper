### Reproduced SES announced multiple control

The FAQ's first USD column is used; its web extraction duplicates the column header, so the second numeric column is deliberately unused. Inputs below are from S2. Source-supported 2024E target adjusted EBITDA is $870–900m; excluding noncash revenue, $680–710m. Synergy NPV is approximately$2,600m. The FAQ uses an assumed €1:$1.09; accounting policies were not consistent across the combined pro forma entities.

| Calculation, USD millions | Result | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| 5,033 −2,600 | 2,433 | Numerator after subtracting buyer synergy NPV |
| 2,433 /885 | 2.7492x | Reproduces promoted2.75x at forecast midpoint |
| 2,433 /695 | 3.5007x | Reproduces promoted3.50x with noncash-excluded midpoint |
| 5,033 /885 | 5.6870x | Control without deducting synergy NPV |
| 5,033 /695 | 7.2417x | Same unadjusted EV, different earnings definition |

These controls explain a presentation choice; **5.69–7.24x is not a proposed valuation range**. Neither midpoint is observed closing-year Starlink EBITDA. An acquisition-specific synergy subtraction cannot silently become a market multiple or a compensation deduction. A valid application requires aligned target earnings, asset perimeter, date, lease convention, growth, capex, accounting and risk. Selecting the lowest promoted ratio would conceal these differences.

Asset replacement should be appraised separately from enterprise income value and seller-equity proceeds. Proposed treatment is a cost-to-recreate inventory with generation, condition, obsolescence and rights constraints, tested against economic recoverability; do not add that inventory value to a DCF that already values its cash generation. Acquisition cash cost, transition expenditure, ongoing public subsidy and legally determined compensation require separate reconciliations. These are proposed analytical controls, not a new conclusion about existing legal entitlements.
