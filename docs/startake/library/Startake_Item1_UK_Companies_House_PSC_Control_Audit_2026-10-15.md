## Assessment

**Evidence gain:** The entity, active status, regulator-reported individual PSC and published nature-of-control, directors, and several dated filings are now tied to concrete original Companies House record URLs. This gives a more precise UK legal-entity/control lead than an unsourced SpaceX/Starlink corporate-chart assertion.

**Competing interpretation and caution:** PSC reports are based on information filed to the registrar. Companies House explicitly warns it does not check the accuracy of all filed information. The current-rendered PSC category and an indexed excerpt differed on the voting-control band: the directly opened source page takes priority for *this observation*, but neither discrepancy nor filing history is resolved without inspecting the dated actual PSC04 PDFs. A UK company identifier and a PSC report cannot validate *ultimate multinational beneficial ownership*, prove a share-sale chain, or show the exact legal owners of space objects, UK gateway sites, Ofcom licences, patents, IP or Starshield-related facilities. Do not infer current **asset ownership** from legal or beneficial **control**.
