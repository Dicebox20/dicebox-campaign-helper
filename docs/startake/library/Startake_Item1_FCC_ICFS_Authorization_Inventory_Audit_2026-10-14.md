## Transfer requirements and status classification

The applicable baseline is **47 CFR §25.119**, and FCC application processes in **§25.110 / Form 312 ICFS**, under statutory FCC authority including 47 U.S.C. §§301, 309, 310. A **substantive assignment or transfer of control** ordinarily needs the FCC public-interest approval process. Statutory public acquisition or asset conveyance does not independently prove that these station grants transfer. There may be limited pro forma and other exceptions, but without buyer legal form, seller corporate title, control details and exact transaction terms these cannot be invoked across the board. See https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-25/section-25.119 and https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/25.110. **Current October 2026 consolidated regulatory text and any post-publication rule changes were not independently validated in native eCFR during this pass.** A December 5, 2025 Federal Register proposal, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-12-05/html/2025-22019.htm, must be classified as a **proposal**, not operative law unless a later final rule is separately confirmed.

Classification rules: `GRANTED conditioned` is not unrestricted, `PARTIALLY GRANTED/DEFERRED` is not fully granted, `STA` is bounded to stated period/conditions, `DISMISSED` is not a license, and `PENDING`/`ACCEPTED FOR FILING` is not granted authority. Satellite `S` callsigns and ground `E` callsigns should not be collapsed into one owner. Authorizations are regulatory rights, distinct from patent, satellite/terminal title, equipment and subscriber-contract ownership.
