## Finding

The latest located primary FCC record still treats the existing Part 25 rules as operative and Part 100 as not yet effective. FCC 26-47 adopted the Part 100 framework and directed the Space Bureau to announce its effective date by public notice. FCC 26-51 said on 7 August 2026 that the new rules were not yet in effect. The Commission's 14 September 2026 final FY 2026 regulatory-fee rule said it would continue its existing approach to space and earth-station fees until Part 100 becomes effective and replaces Part 25. A targeted search of official FCC and Federal Register records through 16 October did not locate a later Part 100 effective-date public notice.

This supports a **bounded inference** that Part 25 remained the operative rule set through the latest official status located; the search does not prove that no later notice exists. The September 14 Federal Register item is the FY 2026 regulatory-fee rule, effective that day; it is not the effective-date notice for Part 100. Accordingly, the existing Item 3 disposal report's Part 25 treatment remains appropriate as of this review cutoff. The Part 100 framework has been adopted, but its future effective date and any later transition action must be rechecked before closing or updating a transaction plan.
