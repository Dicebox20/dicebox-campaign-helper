## Counterevidence / adverse interpretation

An express congressional purchase law does not eliminate just-compensation challenges, foreign vetoes, creditor claims, FCC consent questions, continuity risk, speech/constitutional questions or downstream fiscal burdens. Rule 71.1's breadth does **not** enlarge whatever property interests Congress may constitutionally authorize taking. Conversely, the narrow scope of 40 U.S.C. §§ 3113–3114 does **not** prove federal eminent domain over personal or intangible property could never be granted under a *different valid substantive enactment*. Both errors must be avoided.
