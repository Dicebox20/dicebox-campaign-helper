## Mandatory naming and reference rule — effective October 14, 2026

**The item number is a checklist position, not the research subject's name.** Refer to each study by its **full descriptive subject title**, optionally prefixed with its foundation (Legal or Moral). Do **not** call a document or research job merely “Topic 4,” “Topic 03,” or “Subject #4.”

- **When the user says “item 4 on the checklist”:** locate the **fourth numbered entry in this master tracker**, namely **Telecommunications, Satellite, and Spectrum Status (Legal)**. Do not substitute the fourth subsection, an unrelated research document, or an item from a different checklist.
- **When requesting research:** identify both the checklist item ID and the full descriptive subject, e.g., “Checklist item 04 — Telecommunications, Satellite, and Spectrum Status (Legal).” In the resulting report, the **main title must be the descriptive subject only**, without a numeric “Topic” prefix.
- **Numbered filenames are legacy file IDs and stable relative links**, not subject names. Keep existing filenames and numbering so older cross-references do not break. New *display titles*, handoffs, citations to related subjects, and project communications must be subject-title-first.
- **Deep Research is required for every checklist item** under the project’s existing research rule. Research requests must pass the exact checklist entry title and subcategory scope. A launched research job is not a completion event.
- **Multiple chats:** obtain the most recent tracker and subject work before writing status; reconcile competing updates rather than overwriting newer evidence. If the prior status cannot be verified, record that uncertainty without marking an item complete.

**Examples:** “Checklist item 03 — Statutory Acquisition Procedure and Property Remedies (Legal)” and “Subject: Statutory Acquisition Procedure and Property Remedies.” Never: “Topic 3.”

## Update protocol

1. **Starting a checklist item:** Change its `Status: Not started` to `Status: In progress`; leave its checkbox unchecked.
2. **During research:** Add primary sources, source citations, competing interpretations, open issues, and verification notes to the subject’s separate research document. Record any unresolved evidence gaps there.
3. **Completion gate:** Check the box `[x]` only after all constituent subcategories have been reviewed; claims are sourced; counterarguments and limitations are documented; and substantive open questions are either resolved or explicitly identified.
4. **Update counts:** Recalculate the completed/remaining totals in the progress table after changing checkboxes.
5. **Scope revisions:** If new subcategories are created, update the applicable subject document, its master index, and the 311-subcategory total above. Preserve the 36 checklist item positions unless explicitly approved.
6. **Handoffs:** Keep this tracker next to the `starlink_research_topics/` and `starlink_moral_research_topics/` directories. Relative links will then remain functional. Add a dated log entry below after each work session.
7. **Cross-cutting deliverables:** Maintain C1 independently, use its linked protocol for evidence standards, and avoid treating it as an additional primary research subject or subcategory.
