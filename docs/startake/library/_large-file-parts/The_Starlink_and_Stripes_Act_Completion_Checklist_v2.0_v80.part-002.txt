### What stage means

- **Stage 0 — No dedicated output verified:** no completed source-backed work product confirmed for this deliverable.
- **Stage 1 — Scoped:** scope, requirements, and plan exist, but source review is incomplete or not established.
- **Stage 2 — Evidence foundation:** a relevant saved research report is documented; significant gaps may remain.
- **Stage 3 — Drafted:** a deliverable-specific section, model, or proposed statutory provisions exist and are linked to evidence.
- **Stage 4 — Internally validated:** authenticated original criteria crosswalked; material factual/legal gaps either resolved or expressly treated with defensible bounded assumptions and audit notes.
- **Stage 5 — Congressional-review ready:** consolidated, source-checked, internally reviewed and prepared for required independent professional scrutiny; external expert sign-off recorded as complete only when actually obtained.

No stage requires pretending that currently inaccessible nonpublic records have been acquired. Unresolved gaps must remain visible; Stage 4/5 requires a defensible way of handling them, not their concealment. A stage score is a dashboard aid, distinct from formal 7/36 acceptance.
