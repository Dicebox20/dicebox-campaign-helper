## Executive proposal

Create a wholly public satellite communications operator and authorize a lawful, compensated acquisition of the separable commercial Starlink business. Negotiate first, preserve service throughout the transition and secure essential launch, manufacturing, software and foreign permissions. Use the narrowly drafted domestic-property condemnation fallback only with explicit statutory authority, judicial process and full compensation. Protect all customers against political discrimination and unauthorized surveillance, and make core service and privacy duties enforceable in court.

The 31 sections below form five integrated titles: acquisition (101–110); operator and rights (201–208); finance (301–303); transition and review (401–404); judicial review and effectiveness (501–503), plus sections 1–3. These are substantive proposed provisions. Missing asset schedules, valuation, dollar appropriations, factual findings and professional redlines remain blocking inputs. A draft is progress, not evidence that its conditions can be met.

## Legal anchors and design choices

**Government action and expression.** In [Lebron v. National Railroad Passenger Corp., 513 U.S. 374 (21 February 1995)](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep513/usrep513374/usrep513374.pdf), the Court held that a specially created corporation serving governmental objectives with permanent government power to appoint most directors is governmental for First Amendment purposes. It did not decide the ultimate advertisement dispute. The proposed charter expressly accepts public duties rather than relying on corporate form to avoid them.

**Records and privacy.** [5 U.S.C. §552(f)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t5/s552/) includes government corporations in FOIA's agency definition, with statutory exemptions. [5 U.S.C. §552a(a)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t5/s552a/) defines individuals and systems of records with limits; it is not a worldwide customer privacy code. Sections 201 and 204 therefore expressly apply agency duties and create additional all-customer statutory protections. Those new protections and the section 207 cause of action are policy proposals, not existing customer remedies.

**Corporate finances.** [31 U.S.C. chapter 91](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t31/) sets the government-corporation framework; the current title index identifies §§9101–9108 on definition, creation, budgets, audits, reports, accounts and obligations. Section 201 explicitly applies that framework rather than assuming an unformed entity is already listed. Section 208 is a NEW permanent appropriation of actual operating-account receipts; section 301 merely authorizes later appropriations. Independent fiscal counsel/CBO must assess the interaction, scorekeeping, revenue treatment, apportionment and implementation. No borrowing power or empty-account spending authority is proposed.

**Security and cost.** [NIST CSF 2.0, released 26 February 2024](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2024/02/nist-releases-version-20-landmark-cybersecurity-framework) emphasizes governance and supply-chain risk. [GAO-20-195G, 12 March 2020](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-20-195g) calls for documented technical baselines, assumptions and cost-risk analysis. These frameworks inform proposed gates; neither is evidence the Starlink network passed a test or a binding acquisition-specific approval.

**Acquisition limits.** The [prior acquisition-title report](The_Starlink_and_Stripes_Act_Acquisition_Title_Working_Draft_v0.1_2026-10-15.md) contains externally checked statutory sources, adverse interpretations and caveats incorporated into this bill. In particular, [40 U.S.C. §3114](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title40-section3114) is land-specific, and [47 U.S.C. §310(d)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A47+section%3A310+edition%3Aprelim%29) separately governs relevant transfer permissions. The new text is not a determination that orbital assets, patents, foreign sites or the complete commercial network can be compelled under existing law.
