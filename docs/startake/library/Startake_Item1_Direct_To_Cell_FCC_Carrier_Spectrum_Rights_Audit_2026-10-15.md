## Executive finding

The FCC granted Space Exploration Holdings LLC conditional satellite SCS authority based partly on a **separate approved spectrum-leasing relationship with T-Mobile**, which the FCC identifies as holding the requisite PCS-G co-channel terrestrial licenses across the covered U.S. geographic areas. **The Starlink SCS authorization does not mean SpaceX owns T-Mobile's terrestrial spectrum licenses.** An acquisition must separately diligence the satellite license and the leasing agreement's duration, assignments, carrier/ULS licenses, renewal/consent terms, geographic rights, and handset authorizations. The statutory/regulatory agreement mechanism is an operational dependency, not prima facie fee-simple ownership of spectrum.

## Contrary case and gaps

2. The approval of PCS-G leasing is evidence of licensed collaboration, not evidence SpaceX bought, controls by equity, or can transfer T-Mobile's spectrum. Avoid capitalizing spectrum as wholly owned commercial Starlink property.
3. Locate original approved lease instruments/ULS records, FCC satellite license status, contractual change-of-control rights, carrier economics and restrictions, geographical interference obligations and current regulatory text.
