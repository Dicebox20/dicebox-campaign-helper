## Objective

Produce an independently reviewable **standalone valuation of the Starlink communications business and an associated multi-year acquisition/operating financial model**, expressly excluding unrelated SpaceX launch and other business lines and without assuming Starshield is being acquired. Distinguish fair-value analysis from cash requirements, financing costs, and broader public-benefit arguments.

## Research and model work checklist

- [ ] Fix valuation date, perimeter, ownership and separable economic/legal assets; document Starlink/Connectivity segment/SpaceX-parent differences.
- [ ] Obtain audited or publicly filed current and historic Starlink-relevant consumer, enterprise and government revenue, costs, operating income/EBITDA, cash flow, capex, assets and liabilities. Explicitly allocate shared/parent-segment costs with evidence-based methods.
- [ ] Reconcile the commercial Starlink network and services versus Starshield and other excluded defense or launch business lines, including shared spacecraft, software, factories, workforce and contracts.
- [ ] Model service subscribers, ARPU/price, churn, gross margin, operating expenses, deployment/maintenance, satellite lifetimes, replenishment, replacement launches, terminal supply chain, spectrum/network costs, insurance and growth capex.
- [ ] Produce **discounted cash flow**, **market comparable**, **transaction comparable if available**, and **asset/replacement-cost** valuation scenarios; state assumptions, limitations and relevant compensation-law distinctions.
- [ ] Account for assignability/licensing constraints, transferable IP and software, contracts, taxes, labor retention, transition service agreements, debt, contingent liabilities, employee equity and required working capital.
- [ ] Separate (a) historical government procurement revenue and subsidies, (b) unrealized announced awards and indirect benefits, (c) remaining investment funding and financing cost, and (d) value accruing to private owners. Public payments do not automatically become acquisition credits or personal wealth.
- [ ] Show base / downside / upside sensitivity cases for discount rate, margins, capex, regulation, coverage, demand, launch access, cyber interruptions, and government contracting; include explicit uncertainty bands and counterevidence.
- [ ] Build a **10-year pro forma** with sources/uses, operating cash flow, recurring capex, acquisition/transition cash outlay, debt service where modeled, and budget-scoring caveats. Do not confuse enterprise value, equity value, and total public fiscal cost.
- [ ] Reconcile with a **separate legal just-compensation analysis** and independent financial/legal reviewer; report material source gaps and what cannot be valued reliably.

## Exit deliverables

1. Versioned Starlink-only asset-and-entity perimeter and an audited/source-auditable data appendix.
2. Transparent financial workbook (assumptions, formulas, source/date per variable, and sensitivities).
3. Standalone valuation **range** by method and comparable scenarios; exclude unsupported headline estimates.
4. Multi-year operating and financing forecast, separately stating purchase price, transition costs and public fiscal burden.
5. Independent-review memo: material unknowns, strongest contrary assumptions, sensitivity to SpaceX dependencies, and explicit caveats.
