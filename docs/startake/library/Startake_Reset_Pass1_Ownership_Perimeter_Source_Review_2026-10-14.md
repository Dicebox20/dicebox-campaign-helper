## What the first pass established

1. **Core conclusion:** The likely transaction is a **multi-entity carve-out** with a Starlink-only asset and operating-rights schedule, rather than simple acquisition of stock in a proven standalone Starlink corporation.
2. **Key source:** The SEC-filed 2026 bridge loan provides a valuable candidate asset taxonomy and restrictive-covenant leads, not a substitute for property-title evidence.
3. **Key correction to older report:** SEC Connectivity financials are **not automatically commercial-Starlink-only**, because Starshield contract revenue can be reported within the segment.
4. **First-phase research evidence is materially useful and independently rechecked** on the source types above. This does not resolve a full global entity census, UCC liens, current authorizations, IP title, current EchoStar closing, or all nine original topic subcategories.
