## Open follow-up

The following remain unverified and outside this pass: any actual proclamation or invocation; the identity and jurisdiction of affected operators, stations, or devices; an application to Starlink; and contrary authority or case law.

The OLRC explains that non-positive-law Code titles are prima facie evidence; the underlying enacted Statutes at Large govern if the compiled text conflicts with the statute. This report therefore records the official Code-text comparison and is not an independent review of every underlying enactment.
