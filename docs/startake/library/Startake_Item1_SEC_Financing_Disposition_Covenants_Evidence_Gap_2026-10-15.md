## Question and source index

Does a publicly accessible, actually filed SpaceX financing instrument constrain voluntary disposition or separation of Starlink, and what evidence remains unavailable before declaring assets transferable or unencumbered?

- **F1 — Primary SEC Exhibit 10.9**, March 2, 2026 Bridge Loan Credit Agreement, execution version, **Space Exploration Technologies Corp. borrower**, guarantors described as certain subsidiaries, Goldman Sachs Bank USA administrative agent and other listed financial institutions: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026036936/exhibit109-sx1.htm . Cover identifies parties; §7.03(c) describes restrictions concerning Starlink, Material Starlink Assets, relevant Starlink Financing Assets, Starlink IP licences, spin-off and third-party control, subject to agreement-specific exceptions. This verifies contractual text, **not** a government-imposed restriction or a concluded legal opinion.
- **F2 — Primary alternative SEC exhibit**, another posted version (Exhibit 10.11): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026021860/filename16.htm . §7.03(c) includes express carve-outs referencing §7.02(s)/(t) and an IPO clause marked `[reserved]`, illustrating why version, execution status, amendment and supersession comparisons are required. **Do not silently substitute this text for the March 2 contract.**
- **F3 — SEC public subsequent exhibit**, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026039276/exhibit1010-sx1a1.htm . Contains a substantially related fundamental-changes clause. The specific operative contract and current amendments must be confirmed before any definitive consent result.
- **F4 — Primary corporate financial report**, SpaceX Form 10-Q as of June 30, 2026, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026052535/spcx-20260630.htm . Connectivity is not an asset-title or liens inventory; Starshield overlap blocks simple commercial-only separation.

## Findings and competing conclusions

| Question | Status and supported finding | What is **not** established |
|---|---|---|
| Borrower/counterparties | Exhibit 10.9 identifies Space Exploration Technologies Corp. as borrower; subsidiary guarantors and specified finance counterparties exist in the agreement | Every actual obligor, all executed signature pages and presently effective amendments not reconciled |
| Contractual transfer conditions | §7.03(c) contains a substantial restriction on transactions resulting in third-party disposition/control of Starlink or specified material assets, including certain licences and spin-offs | Blanket prohibition of every purchase, actual lender consent vote, present contractual default, or automatic invalidity of a later deal |
| Financing-asset descriptions | Defined categories in the agreement are useful **diligence leads** for receivables, IP, contracts and financing assets | A signed transaction asset schedule, asset-level ownership, absence/presence/perfection of liens, patent title, or a full subsidiary tree |
| Different financing texts | SEC hosts more than one similar agreement/exhibit and the clauses/exceptions must be version-controlled | That any one historical public version is necessarily the currently operative unamended agreement |
| Public acquisition consequence | A voluntary deal would require counsel to inspect operative provisions, permitted exceptions, security and release mechanics. A hypothetical compulsory acquisition requires a distinct statutory/constitutional analysis | That private covenants categorically defeat Congress's authority or excuse any just compensation |

## Method and limitations

Official SEC originals were consulted as dated contractual disclosures, but no certified current note register, company collateral schedule, executed amendments audit, debtor-specific UCC search, lender confirmation, legal opinion, or individual asset transfer consent was obtained. Different versions of similar financing documents constitute **version-control risk**, not proof that restrictions currently disappeared. This scoped pass improves contract-specific evidence, but **does not** certify the target's legal title, creditor clearance, asset acquisition perimeter or acquisition feasibility.
