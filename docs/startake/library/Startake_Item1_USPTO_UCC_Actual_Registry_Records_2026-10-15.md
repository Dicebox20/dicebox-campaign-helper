## Practical implications and negative findings

- **Record-level progress, not title certification.** The patent numbers and one reel/frame identifier give concrete targets for official registry pull-through. They are supported by indexed secondary source renderings, not direct inspection of the USPTO-recorded assignments. The original official interface's existence is not itself a result from that interface.
- **A Bank of America recorded release** appearing against individual patent family records does not establish that every SpaceX or Starlink patent, terminal, satellite, receivable, or other asset is unencumbered; identify each release's covered instruments, debtor, collateral, and continuing effect.
- **Corporate conversion** (Delaware to Texas, indexed as February 2024 filing with February 2025 assignment record) is relevant to historical debtor name/jurisdiction and patent-owner record matching; an official entity certificate and historical names need validation separately.
- **UCC is not entirely a public free-text web export.** Texas presently requires account/payment; no payment is authorized or made. Certified searches are not available in this pass. Also inspect proper Article 9 debtor location, prior states/names, local fixtures, Delaware/Texas history, and applicable non-UCC federal IP recording.
- **No standalone Starlink beneficial ownership conclusion.** Patent assignee Space Exploration Technologies Corp. is not synonymous with legal title to every commercial Starlink communications asset, and disclosed SpaceX Connectivity results include some Starshield activity. Shared use, classified interfaces, IP licences and lender agreements remain unverified.
