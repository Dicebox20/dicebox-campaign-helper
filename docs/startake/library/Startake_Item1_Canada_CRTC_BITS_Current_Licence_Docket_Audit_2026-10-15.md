## Primary-source findings

1. **Original licence granted, but enclosure missing.** The CRTC letter dated **15 October 2020**, reference **8190-S206-202002799**, expressly approves a BITS application made by **Space Exploration Technologies Corp.**, stating that a licence is enclosed, with conditions on its reverse side and regular-filing obligations. The public HTML renders the approval letter **without the enclosed signed licence**, its licence number, expiry/renewal record or full back-page conditions. The letter warns that this licence does not itself authorize facilities-based or non-facilities-based service-provider operation.
2. **A distinct original procedural record.** The CRTC's **6 July 2020** letter associates the registration request (filed 1 May 2020) and opportunity for public interventions with the same docket. The CRTC's **22 June 2020** procedural letter documents OneWeb's extension request; the CRTC's 2020 commission letters index includes the 15 October and 6 July letters under the same file number. This corroborates the original docket and applicant; it does **not** constitute a later assignment-history search.
3. **Current BITS records have a separate public search mechanism, but native record not retrieved.** CRTC's BITS licensees landing page links its application-viewing portal, provides pre-2020 year links and describes the Form 503 application and affidavit procedure. During this pass, the application portal returned **502 Bad Gateway**, and an attempted current 2020 year-list URL could not be retrieved. A current certified/native record matched to this docket, holder, licence status, renewals and transfer-history therefore **was not obtained**.
4. **Relevant later enforcement decision, not a status certificate.** CRTC Telecom Decision **2026-94 (20 May 2026)** revokes BITS licences of 50 expressly listed entities for failure to keep required records current. Neither **Space Exploration Technologies Corp.** nor the original **8190-S206-202002799** docket appears in its published appendix. This is evidence only of **non-inclusion in that specific revocation order**, **not affirmative proof of an active licence**, no other surrender, later revocation, expiry or assignment.
5. **Transfer and name-change are distinguishable filing events.** CRTC's 2026 Data Collection System form index distinguishes **Form 504 — BITS Licence Surrender; Form 505 — BITS Licence Name Change; Form 506 — Transfer BITS Licence to another Entity; Form 507 — Receipt of BITS Licence from another Entity**. No completed Form 505/506/507, associated order, or other intervening transaction instrument was found in this bounded review. Mere appearance of **SpaceX Canada Corp.** in other Canadian records does not prove an assignment.
6. **Regulatory relevance.** CRTC's 2026 provider-registration guidance reiterates that entities carrying international telecom traffic need a BITS licence. The original licence and its current state must be individually established rather than inferred from ISED gateway authorizations, accessibility indexes or a 2020 grant alone.

## Contradictions and evidentiary constraints

- **Historical issuance versus current good standing:** The Oct 2020 approval has high confidence for original grant, but zero independent current-status proof from a native register extract. No negative inference from the portal outage.
- **2026 non-revocation in one list:** Absence from Decision 2026-94 cannot exclude other revocations, surrenders, nonrenewal, expiry, amendments or change-of-holder. Do not label the original licence current/active on this basis.
- **Licence-holder versus service/operator/corporate chain:** The original named BITS applicant is not proof of the same contemporary CRTC telecommunications provider entity, customer contracting entity or asset owner. The CA-G02, G03 and G05 tracks remain separate.
- **Procedural and attached instrument gap:** The grant letter refers to a physical/attached licence and terms that were not exposed by the reviewed HTML. No original signed instrument, condition sheet, complete proceeding file, or Form 505–507 history authenticated.
