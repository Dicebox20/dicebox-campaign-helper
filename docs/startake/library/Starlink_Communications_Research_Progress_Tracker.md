## Update protocol

1. **Starting a topic or an original eight-step checklist item:** Assign substantive investigation to the **Deep Research app** as a mandatory project rule. Change `Status: Not started` to `Status: Deep Research initiated` and leave its checkbox unchecked. Ordinary chat is used for housekeeping and synchronization, not in place of Deep Research.
2. **During research:** Add primary sources, source citations, competing interpretations, open issues, and verification notes to that topic’s separate research document. Record any unresolved evidence gaps there.
3. **Completion gate:** Check the box `[x]` only after all constituent subcategories and the topic’s original exit test have been satisfied; claims are sourced; counterarguments and limitations documented; and material evidence inventories completed or their unavoidable limits explained. A Deep Research first pass or narrative report alone does not complete a topic.
4. **Update counts:** Recalculate the completed/remaining totals in the progress table after changing checkboxes.
5. **Scope revisions:** If new subcategories are created, update the applicable topic document, its master index, and the currently **317-subcategory** total above. Preserve the 36-topic numbering unless explicitly approved.
6. **Handoffs:** Keep this tracker next to the `starlink_research_topics/` and `starlink_moral_research_topics/` directories. Relative links will then remain functional. Add a dated log entry below after each work session.
