## Update protocol for named topics

- **Starting a topic:** Change its `Status: Not started` to `Status: In progress`; leave its checkbox unchecked.
- **During research:** Add primary sources, source citations, competing interpretations, open issues, and verification notes to that topic’s separate research document. Record any unresolved evidence gaps there.
- **Completion gate:** Check the box `[x]` only after all constituent subcategories have been reviewed; claims are sourced; counterarguments and limitations are documented; and substantive open questions are either resolved or explicitly identified.
- **Update counts:** Recalculate the completed/remaining totals in the progress table after changing checkboxes.
- **Scope revisions:** If new subcategories are created, update the applicable topic document, its master index, and the 311-subcategory total above. Preserve the 36-topic count and underlying archived file paths; identify the topics by names rather than numeric labels.
- **Handoffs:** Keep this tracker next to the `starlink_research_topics/` and `starlink_moral_research_topics/` directories. Relative links will then remain functional. Add a dated log entry below after each work session.
- **Cross-cutting deliverables:** Maintain the Public Funding and Wealth Attribution requirement independently, use its linked protocol for evidence standards, and avoid treating it as an additional primary research topic or subcategory.
