The Ofcom report does not clear a separate acquisition-screening regime. **An unchanged licensed company can still be the subject of a notifiable acquisition of control.** This is a synthesis of the preserved Ofcom distinction and the NSI entity-control framework below, not a decision on Starlink's actual transaction. ## Timing and contrary evidence

The July2026 general guidance separates acceptance/review from a called-in assessment: 30 working days for accepted-notification review, then an initial 30-working-day assessment, potentially another45 and agreed extensions. Information/attendance notices pause assessment counting, not the review deadline. [Official guidance, timing and assessment sections](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-security-and-investment-act-guidance-on-acquisitions?lang=en-gb); [official explanatory notes on §23](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/25/notes/division/8/index.htm). This is not a guaranteed total transaction duration. Preparations, rejected filings and remedies can add time.

Material adverse limit: the March12,2026 consultation response proposes amended sector definitions and says secondary legislation is required. It is not itself operative legislation. [Official consultation response](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/consultation-on-the-nsi-act-notifiable-acquisition-regulations/outcome/national-security-and-investment-act-notifiable-acquisition-specification-of-qualifying-entities-regulations-2021-consultation-response). The official collection, updated October12, still links the 17-sector instrument and that response. [Current official collection](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/national-security-and-investment-act?lang=en-gb).

A secondary vLex search result labels an instrument version “in vigour from 2026-09-30.” That is a **conflicting currency lead, not relied-on legal authority**. Official consolidated contents/change-history access failed; searches did not authenticate an amending instrument or explain the label. The inspected enacted PDF and guidance are therefore a documented baseline, **not certification that every October2026 amendment has been checked**. Before transaction reliance, obtain the current consolidated regulations, amendment/commencement history and counsel reconciliation. Do not silently promote either the consultation or the secondary date into law.
