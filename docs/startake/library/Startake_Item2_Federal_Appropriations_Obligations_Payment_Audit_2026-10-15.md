# STARTAKE — Item 2: Appropriations, obligations, and lawful payment controls

**Research date:** 2026-10-15 UTC. **Verified claim:** `STARTAKE-20261015-item2-appropr-38c6f2` (tracker v81). **Method:** One bounded direct official statutory / GAO primary-source review; not Deep Research or an independent legal opinion. **Acquisition perimeter:** hypothetical congressionally authorized, compensated acquisition of Starlink commercial communications assets/operations only, not the whole of SpaceX or presumptively Starshield.

## Finding

Congressional power to authorize a hypothetical purchase and the existence of a legal compensation remedy do not establish that a federal agency currently has appropriations available to obligate or pay for buying the Starlink enterprise. The general statutory framework requires legally available **purpose, time and amount** authority, plus appropriate documentary support for obligations. Neither previously reviewed emergency communications controls, DPA rated orders, nor the Tucker Act money-claims forum independently supplies an identifiable funded Starlink asset-acquisition appropriation. A hypothetical special acquisition law could specify distinct substantive authorization and funding/transfer/payment controls; no enacted Starlink-specific law or amount is identified in this pass.

## Alternative interpretations, legal boundaries, and contradictions

- **Authorization vs appropriation:** An agency may have a program objective or legal acquisition authority yet lack budget authority to obligate a particular purchase. Conversely, funds existing in a defense/broadband account do not by themselves provide a lawful title-vesting mechanism or an appropriate purpose.
- **Asset purchase vs judgments:** §1304 can fund certain final judgments, but not automatically the entire up-front purchase of satellites, spectrum operating rights, related contracts and privately owned gateways. §1491 defines some claims jurisdiction, not a bank account for voluntary acquisition.
- **Obligations vs outlays:** A recorded valid obligation is not necessarily payment already disbursed. The historic public-funding ledger under Item 4 tracks a different direction of money flow and does not establish a future appropriations source for acquiring Starlink.
- **Compensation and owners:** For compelled property acquisition, compensation, claims and appropriation design will depend on legally cognizable property interests, title holders, creditor priority, authorizing statute and final adjudications. No specific congressional Starlink acquisition measure was identified.
