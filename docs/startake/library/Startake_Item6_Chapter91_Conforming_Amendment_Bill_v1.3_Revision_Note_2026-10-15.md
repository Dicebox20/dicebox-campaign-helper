## Change made

Bill v1.3 adds proposed subsection §201(d) under “Establishment and Public Ownership.” It retains the bill's direct creation clause in §201(a) and its Chapter 91 treatment in §201(c), then amends 31 U.S.C. §9101(3) to list the United States Public Satellite Communications Corporation as new subparagraph (Q). Because the current terminal subparagraph (P) ends with a period, §201(d) first changes that period to “; and” before adding (Q), so the list reads continuously. The official preliminary 2026 text reviewed for the prior audit ends §9101(3) at (P), “the International Clean Energy Foundation.” ([31 U.S.C. § 9101](https://usc-cdn.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title31-section9101).)

The text added to Bill v1.3 is:

> **(d) CONFORMING AMENDMENT TO CHAPTER 91.—** Section 9101(3) of title 31, United States Code, is amended—  
> (1) in subparagraph (P), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and  
> (2) by adding at the end the following:  
> “(Q) the United States Public Satellite Communications Corporation established by section 201 of this Act.”

## Source basis and limits

The prior Item 6 audit found that proposed §201(a), if enacted, directly establishes a named, federally owned corporation and likely satisfies 31 U.S.C. §9102's specific-law requirement. Section 9102 does not itself authorize a Starlink acquisition or condemnation. Bill v1.3 therefore leaves §201(a) and the separate acquisition provisions unchanged. ([31 U.S.C. § 9102](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A31+section%3A9102+edition%3Aprelim%29); prior [Item 6 Chapter 91 classification and charter audit](Startake_Item6_31_USC_Chapter91_Corporation_Classification_Charter_Audit_2026-10-15.md), Library ID `libfile_ab9a0468d9ac819184bf5de1ed114d9e`.)

The audit also concluded that §201(c)'s existing Chapter 91-specific deeming clause likely supplies coverage even though the current §9101(3) list does not yet include this proposed entity. The strongest argument against requiring a conforming amendment is therefore that §201(c) may already be sufficient as a specific statutory rule. The new §201(d) is a codification clarification to align the list with the express bill text, not a finding that §201(c) is defective. No controlling decision on this exact interaction was identified in the bounded audit. ([31 U.S.C. §§ 9101–9102](https://usc-cdn.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title31-section9101); [31 U.S.C. § 9102](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A31+section%3A9102+edition%3Aprelim%29).)

The official preliminary 2026 Code text reviewed for the prior audit states its §9101 text contains laws in effect on 28 September 2026. The use of “(Q)” is based on that list; confirm the current subparagraph lettering before bill introduction. No claim is made about amendments after that Code cutoff.
