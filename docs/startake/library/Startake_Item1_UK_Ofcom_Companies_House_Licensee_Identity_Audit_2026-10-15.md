## Executive finding

**The seemingly inconsistent names identify different registered corporations, and at least one relevant Ofcom licence is issued directly to each.** Ofcom's executed-looking, public regulator-hosted original network licence **1239247/3**, dated 6 February 2024, expressly identifies **STARLINK INTERNET SERVICES LIMITED** with an **Ireland/Dublin** licensee address. The official Irish CRO's gazette records exact Irish company **677409** as **STARLINK INTERNET SERVICES LIMITED**; a third-party supplier's published contractual terms explicitly links Irish company 677409 to the same exact legal name. By contrast, the regulator-hosted original Isle of Man gateway licence **1249304/3**, dated 16 October 2024, expressly names **Starlink Internet Services UK Limited** at **7 Albemarle Street, London, W1S 4HQ**, matching UK Companies House **12794964**. The UK and Ireland legal entities must not be merged in a conveyance schedule merely because both operate the Starlink network, share branding, or may be under common control.

This audit **reconciles the legal names and country of incorporation at primary-record level**, with an additional contractual cross-reference supporting the exact Irish registration. It is **not** a complete share-ownership audit, a certified live complete licence inventory, proof of satellite/site real-property ownership, a corporate-group relationship certificate, or proof that an acquisition would convey either licence. The underlying title, consent, payment, regulatory and intercompany arrangements remain unverified. Research pass complete **with open gaps**; formal Item 1 and original named-topic acceptance remain **No**.

### Contradictions and resolution

- **Apparent name mismatch:** “... Limited” and “... UK Limited” previously appeared to be a typographical inconsistency. Native Ofcom licence PDFs and country-specific corporate registers support **two distinct legal persons**, not aliases.
- **Different Dublin addresses:** Ofcom's February 2024 version names Beaux Lane House, while EE's December 2025 contract names Miesian Plaza; this is consistent with a subsequent address change but **the actual CRO change-of-address history has not been verified**. Do not assert date or cause of move.
- **English vs Welsh Ofcom register content:** March 2026 Welsh page lists the UK-IoM licence, whereas updated English page omits it. The actual 2024 native licence PDF is publicly retrievable. **Omission is an inventory reconciliation issue, not proof of cancellation.**
- **2024 Ofcom variation narrative:** refers generically to the Starlink name for four sites, despite the IoM native issued licence naming the UK company. Licence-holder identity must be established instrument-by-instrument.
