## Executive findings and source-level evidence

1. **Official Nova Scotia name-change record establishes historical continuity, not shareholders.** The Nova Scotia Registry of Joint Stock Companies published a Royal Gazette notice in *The Royal Gazette*, 15 July 2020, p. 1112 (PDF page index 5), specifying **old name TIBRO CANADA CORP.; new name SPACEX CANADA CORP.; effective 22 June 2020**. This is stronger than a loose marketing-name inference: it is an official recorded name-change event for a Canadian corporate registrant. The notice as reviewed supplies no underlying incorporation number, historical articles, named directors, issued shares, beneficial owners, ownership percentage or ultimate-control instrument. [CA02-01]
2. **Official Nunavut registry notice identifies jurisdiction and a territorial registration.** The *Nunavut Gazette* (Vol. 25, No. 11, Part I, printed p. 107, PDF page index 6) lists **SpaceX Canada Corp.**, corporation/registration number **ET 12655**, jurisdiction **Nova Scotia**, registration date **17 August 2023**, and a Nunavut registered-office contact through Field LLP / GFY Paralegal Services at Iqaluit. **ET 12655 is a Nunavut registration identifier, not established as Nova Scotia incorporation number or federal corporate number.** The notice does not disclose its equity or directors. [CA02-02]
3. **Alberta King's Printer confirms separate extraprovincial registration.** Alberta's official Corporate Registry *Registrar's Periodical*, 15 August 2025, lists **SPACEX CANADA CORP. — Other Prov/Territory Corps — Registered 2025 JUL 09; Alberta no. 2127288534; registered address 3400, 350 7th Avenue SW, Calgary AB T2P 3N9**. That is a province-level registration, **not** the original incorporation event, evidence of current equity ownership or gateway title. [CA02-03]
4. **A native CRTC 2019 index connects Canadian name history to a different BITS docket.** CRTC's official *BITS 8190: 2019* index includes application **8190-T230-201902883** dated **26 April 2019**, displayed holder **“SpaceX Canada Corp. (Formerly TIBRO Canada Corp.)”**, status **Approved / Name change**, and an updated period shown **23 July 2020 to 30 June 2029** (the older period, 27 May 2019 to 30 June 2029, is struck through). That supports the *Tibro → SpaceX Canada* continuity and identifies a separate earlier Canadian-company BITS record. **This is distinct from the 15 October 2020 BITS approval of Space Exploration Technologies Corp. in docket 8190-S206-202002799**, previously reviewed under CA-G01. Do not merge the two dockets, infer a transfer between entities or treat the index as a corporate share register. The updated CRTC index materially augments the earlier CRTC research and should be cross-checked in a future CA-G01 native-instrument review. [CA02-04; historical CA-G01 report]
5. **A 2026 SpaceX SEC-filed Canadian prospectus provides a functional agency relationship, not a shareholding chart.** The filing identifies **SpaceX Canada Corp.** as the Canadian agent for service of process for **Space Exploration Technologies Corp. and named officers/directors**, at **1741 Lower Water Street, Suite 600, Halifax, NS B3J 0J2**. It identifies the parent company's material-subsidiary table (CTC Property LLC, SpaceX Services Inc., X Corp., X.AI LLC), **but does not identify SpaceX Canada Corp. in that limited table**. Agent-of-service designation proves a documented legal representation function, **not** ownership percentages or absence of subsidiary status (the table is *material* subsidiaries, not a universal list). The same filing dates the parent company's Texas reincorporation to February 14, 2024. [CA02-05]
6. **Canadian government lobbying and import datasets corroborate operating-party identity, not incorporation ownership.** The Office of the Commissioner of Lobbying identifies **SpaceX Canada Corp.** as an in-house corporation registrant (entry shown active from 27 March 2026, posted 14 May 2026), and lists **Gwynne Shotwell, President and COO** as *responsible officer* (a lobbying disclosure designation; **not a certified company-director appointment**). ISED's Canadian Importers Database lists SPACEX CANADA CORP. at **Halifax, Nova Scotia B3J 0J2** for certain products; import data does not establish issued capital or title to Starlink equipment. [CA02-06; CA02-07]

### Contrary evidence and interpretation controls

- **Not the same licence holder by name.** A CRTC 2020 approval in the original CA-G01 report names **Space Exploration Technologies Corp.**; the **2019** CRTC index names **SpaceX Canada Corp., formerly Tibro Canada Corp.** Separate dockets and names must be preserved, not conflated. An amendment/name-change in the Tibro dossier is **not** proof of a conveyance of the U.S. corporate licence.
- **Official document hierarchy:** Nova Scotia Royal Gazette establishes recorded name-change; Nunavut and Alberta records establish registrations outside the home jurisdiction. None provides the **Nova Scotia originating formation number, current directors, classes or issued shares**, or an authenticated look-through to ultimate owners.
- **SEC filing's material-subsidiary list is not exhaustive.** Absence of SpaceX Canada Corp. from the material list is no proof it is independent or unrelated. The same SEC filing expressly designates it as Canadian legal agent, supporting practical relationship rather than ultimate control.
- **Lobbying officer versus company director:** Gwynne Shotwell's lobbying responsible-officer role should not be substituted for a current certified Nova Scotia director filing.
- **Temporal limits:** 2019/2020/2023/2025 dated notices prove the events at those dates; they do not establish unbroken present good standing or unchanged registered office. 2026 public indexes support current-facing usage but not full corporate status certification.
- **No paid registry purchase or external outreach** was made; no user authorization for expenses or communications was assumed.
