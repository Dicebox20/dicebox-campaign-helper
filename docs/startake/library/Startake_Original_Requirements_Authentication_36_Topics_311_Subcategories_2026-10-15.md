## Seven original practical gates — preserved

1. [ ] **Ownership and acquisition perimeter.** Verify precisely which Starlink communications assets, rights, entities, liabilities, and owners would be implicated.

2. [ ] **Constitutional and statutory acquisition authority.** Verify public purpose, authorizing legislation, just compensation, procedural remedies, and limits.

3. [ ] **Independent operation and international permissions.** Verify launch, manufacturing, staffing, operating continuity, international treaty and licensing conditions.

4. [ ] **Verified public-funding ledger.** Trace Starlink-specific actual receipts, obligations, grants, contracts, and indirect support without double-counting SpaceX-wide awards.

5. [ ] **Starlink-only valuation and financial model.** Develop documented, standalone Starlink valuations and financial/cost scenarios; distinguish parent-company value, Starshield, related-party/shared costs, and Musk's personal net worth. [Research scope and evidence checklist](Starlink_Only_Valuation_and_Financial_Model.md).

6. [ ] **Public governance, security, and service protections.** Verify proposed oversight, independent governance, cybersecurity, civil liberties, and critical-service continuity mechanisms.

7. [ ] **Necessity assessment and independent legal review.** Compare acquisition against less intrusive alternatives and subject the legal, financial, and operational case to independent review.

These are original tracker-level requirements, not invented substitutes. The full asset-owner map, current legal authority/compensation analysis, executable transition/permission plans, transaction ledger, independently supported financial workbook/range, implemented safeguards, alternatives and independent review still require evidence. The separate Public Funding and Wealth Attribution protocol remains a cross-cutting requirement, not an eighth practical item or 37th named topic; its complete workstream audit is outside this source-custody assignment.
