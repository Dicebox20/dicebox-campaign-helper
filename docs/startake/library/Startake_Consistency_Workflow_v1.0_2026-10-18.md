## Governing rule

Before changing a package component, identify its authoritative baseline, explain the intended change, identify dependent components, and verify preservation of everything outside scope. A successful save proves persistence; it does not prove factual accuracy, legal sufficiency, or package consistency. Independent readback means a fresh retrieval, not independent professional review.

## 3. Maintain evidence and decision consistency

Each material assertion needs source identity, exact document/section, jurisdiction, publication/effective/retrieval dates, and scope. Distinguish verified fact, disputed account, inference, scenario assumption and unknown. Retrieve and inspect source content; a plausible URL or prior model output is insufficient. Flag superseded statutes, changing statistics and unverified citation currency. A quota limit is a source limitation, not permission to invent evidence.

For a contradiction, open an issue with stable ID, competing propositions and sources, affected provisions/outputs, severity, disposition and unresolved evidence. Do not silently choose the convenient account. Historical reports remain intact; add a dated erratum or superseding finding with links. An adverse finding can reopen a prior conclusion; preserve the old status and reason in history.

Keep a decision entry for each substantive choice: problem, options including contrary arguments, selected approach, supporting evidence, consequences, owner and reopening trigger. Separate enacted law from proposed new authority. Do not copy uncertain findings into an executive summary as established fact.

## 5. Save as a recoverable transaction

1. Preserve the predecessor; create explicitly versioned substantive successors. For canonical tracker/checklist updates retain their stable IDs and exact current filenames. Never overwrite unrelated content or remove a version guard on conflict.
2. Validate candidates locally. Produce the diff, preservation checks, dependency dispositions and remaining issues. Any scope expansion requires a refreshed claim or separate assignment before proceeding.
3. Save report and dependent artifacts in a declared order. Independently retrieve each saved candidate and compare exact bytes/digests with the intended local file. Record each successful ID/revision; a batch may partially succeed.
4. Refresh tracker and actual conditional version. Integrate only owned findings, release only the owned claim, and record the package's exact revisions and any pending synchronization. Verify the saved tracker independently.
5. Refresh checklist, conditionally synchronize its checkpoint, independently verify, then record its verified receipt in the tracker and verify that final tracker update. Update the goal last. Avoid an endless receipt loop: the final tracker readback belongs in the external transaction receipt; it does not require another tracker edit merely to describe its own hash.
6. A package checkpoint is reconciled only after these checks. Before then label it partially saved/integration pending. Do not select the next research scope until the current transaction is reconciled or explicitly blocked under the research workflow.

No claim of multi-file atomicity: there is no guaranteed all-or-nothing save across files. Use the receipt to make partial completion recoverable.

## 6. Failure, interruption and rollback

On interruption, resume from the last independently verified step. Refresh live files and ownership, inspect any uncertain write's actual outcome, and reconcile before retrying. Do not blindly repeat a timed-out upload or release someone else's reservation. Never claim an intended save as completed.

Stop integration for lost ownership, unexpected protected-field changes, unexplained numerical differences, unresolved material authority/source contradictions, or incompatible artifact revisions. Record the blocker and affected files. Unaffected research may continue only through a separate valid reservation when permitted.

For rollback, preserve the failed successor and evidence. Create a corrected successor based on the last verified checkpoint, or conditionally repair the canonical coordinator; record supersession and downstream repairs. Do not restore a stale tracker wholesale over later work. Reopening an accepted criterion requires provenance and justification; do not conceal a regression.

## 7. Periodic package reconciliation

Run dependency review after every substantive integration. After every five substantive integrations, at a phase transition, after a major authority/perimeter/funding change, and before declaring professional-review readiness, reserve a distinct whole-package consistency pass. Compare the current bill, executive proposal, legal memos, model, transition plan, crosswalk and dashboard against one pinned revision set. Reconcile terminology, amounts/units, dates, acquisition route, scope exclusions, remedies, funds and deliverable obligations. This interval is a review trigger, not evidence that a review ran.

Maintain a visible unresolved-issue table. Before readiness, every material issue must be resolved with evidence or explicitly assigned to a concrete external-review blocker. Internal consistency never substitutes for independent legal, appraisal, budget/accounting or engineering review.
