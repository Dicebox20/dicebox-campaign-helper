## Adverse interpretations and substantive limitations

A region/time-slice capacity total is not actual delivered throughput, coverage, latency, uptime, spectrum permission or customer service. Allocations and effective capacities are inputs, not solver-generated engineering facts. Evidence must align direction, geography, concurrency, generation, failure margins and units. The model does not connect the fleet cohorts automatically to regional demand or reduce financial revenue if service fails. That interface requires a documented generation/orbit/service mapping and later integration under separate scope. Time-slice selection itself can conceal outages; independent engineering must select representative peak, maintenance and failure conditions.

Terminal opening_state must exactly equal closing_state, and costs must be declared complete. This compares supplied assertions only: no cohort/working-capital state is automatically simulated or authenticated. Matching incomplete dictionaries does not prove stationarity. Actual evidence must reconcile normalized age distribution, inventory, pipeline, service capacities, continuing rights, working capital and all recurring costs. A finite observed cycle cannot guarantee infinite rights, technology, failure rates or supplier availability. Growth, inflationary nominal prices and changing generations require a different supported model; the existing engine's growth terminal result is not replaced or validated by this companion. Negative values are retained rather than floored away. Source/date/status completeness is not source authentication.
