## Legal and procedural context

Under [47 C.F.R. § 25.120](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/part-25), an STA request may be made for temporary use of facilities; when an extension is sought, the request generally must reach the Commission at least three working days before the existing temporary authorization expires. The rule supplies procedural context, not the missing case status. This audit does not determine whether the successor request was officially received when required or whether the Commission granted it.

[47 C.F.R. § 1.10013](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/part-1) directs applicants to use the Search Tools on the FCC’s ICFS homepage to check an application’s status. The attempted public retrieval of that route did not return the live case record. The eCFR Part 25 page reported Title 47 current through 7 October 2026 when checked on 17 October; no later rule text was relied upon here. [eCFR Part 25](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/part-25).
