# Creative canon: The Mysterious Lodge

**Authority: direct owner statements in the current conversation, 2026-10-10.** The contents below are user-confirmed. Do NOT silently add plot facts from source code, assistants, or other campaigns.

## Identity and influences

- Current title **The Mysterious Lodge**; original title **Central Lodge**.
- **Castle of Dreams** is the prototype for this puzzle-progression campaign, not the same campaign.
- The gameplay inspiration is **Resident Evil 1** and **Devil May Cry**: object-based mystery, linked puzzles, revisiting locations, and progression toward a finale.
- **House of the Damned** is a separate campaign and must not be mixed in.

## Owner-confirmed story

The player characters have returned from the **Feywild**. They pass through a mysterious, confusing forest that leads them to the Lodge. The Lodge is owned by a **lineage of devil-worshipping cultists**. The players must investigate and solve a web of puzzles and mysteries until they reach the final room. There they discover the family **is still alive** and **already summoning their master**. Reaching the ritual is the campaign objective. **What happens after that reveal is undecided.**

## Intended creative experience

The house and grounds should reward observation, item-to-location associations and revisiting previously examined spaces. The mystery should reveal connections gradually rather than give away the family/ritual at the entrance. This last paragraph interprets the owner's stated game influences and goals; it is a **design interpretation**, not a new mandatory scene-level canon claim.

## Intentionally open questions

- The family's names, identities, number of members, present motives beyond devil worship, and actors/tokens at the finale.
- Who exactly their summoning master is; a recovered C1 Scene is titled Shrine of Asmodeus, but that does **not** establish the ritual's master as Asmodeus.
- Whether the ritual succeeds, fails, can be interrupted, or leads to combat, negotiation, escape, or another ending.
- Any definitive chronology of the family's disappearances, victims or infernal pact not personally approved by the owner.
- The particular Feywild event or reasons the forest chooses the party, beyond the owner's stated arrival premise.

When brainstorming, mark suggestions PROPOSED, not APPROVED. Preserve and reference the approved canon even if a source-script implementation tells a slightly different story.
