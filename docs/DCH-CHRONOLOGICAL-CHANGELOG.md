## How to read this changelog

- **Implemented** = source changes supported by a commit/receipt; **built/tested** = tool output or a checkpoint report, not necessarily a live product result.
- **Installed** = source/install checksum or release receipt; **owner-accepted** = a specifically recorded owner-observed behavior, not general product certification.
- A continuity **PASS** indicates the checkpoint passed its recording protocol. A **PARTIAL** may still contain verified evidence; neither means a Roadmap v2 milestone is accepted.
- Dates for commits use author time converted to UTC; turn dates are `startedAtUtc`. Earlier Drive dates identify source-document decisions, not precise execution timestamps.
- Historical M02 map attempts are **not** Roadmap v2 Milestone M2 (automatic diagnostics). Legacy Stage 8/12 records are similarly distinct from current M0–M5.
