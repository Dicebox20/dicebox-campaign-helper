## Cross-cutting Region-triggered Macro permission rule — accepted 2026-10-12
This is a permanent project-wide runtime acceptance gate, not a new campaign phase and not permission to broaden any live-world write. Before installing, enabling, or accepting a RegionBehavior that executes a Macro on a player-triggered event, resolve the exact RegionBehavior and Macro IDs, intended triggering users, and Macro ownership. Every intended player must be able to execute that Macro under Foundry's actual permission model. A RegionBehavior's `everyone=true` setting does not override Macro document permissions; a Macro with default ownership `NONE` can still fail for players. For genuinely player-facing Region-triggered Macros, use default `LIMITED` (1) as the minimum broadly compatible permission unless a verified explicit-per-user policy covers every intended player and future-join behavior. Never grant `OWNER`/edit permission merely to enable execution. After installation, independently reread the Macro and RegionBehavior, verify enabled state and event binding, check `canUserExecute(user)` (or the exact-version equivalent) for the intended player accounts, then repeat the permission readback after a fresh reload. GM-only operations must remain GM-authorized and must not be exposed by elevating player access; route sensitive operations through the existing narrow GM-authorized adapter or keep the trigger GM-only. A failed check blocks acceptance until repaired. This rule applies to all current and future ChatGPT/Codex project sessions through the foundation contract and Foreman workflow.
