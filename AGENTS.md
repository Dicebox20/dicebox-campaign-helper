DiceBox tactical-map camera rule: all player-facing tactical/battle maps default to a strict 90-degree overhead orthographic projection. Never use or accept prompt language such as `isometric`, `top-down/isometric`, `bird's-eye`, `three-quarter`, `angled overhead`, `cinematic aerial`, or other wording that invites perspective tilt. RENDER prompts must explicitly state `strict 90-degree overhead orthographic battlemap, camera perpendicular to the ground plane, no perspective tilt, no horizon`. The current structural style deliberately uses a 3D modeled/Blender-like appearance; depth may be conveyed through wall-top thickness, bevels, object height, material relief, ambient occlusion, and contact shadows, but the camera must not tilt merely to expose side faces. INSPECT rejects perspective/isometric drift even when topology and mood otherwise pass. This camera rule outranks aesthetic phrasing such as cinematic composition.
